**Hit check and the animations** (no browser: the 59 animation specs were fitted numerically, not drawn by eye)

- Method: every spec started from a hand-written offset list on its base builder; a coordinate-descent fitter (scratch, not kept) moved the spec numbers in steps of 0.4, 0.2, 0.1 and 0.05 rad, with a small cost on every change, until all uses of the animation passed the P17-02 hit check limits (short 0.1, side 0.45, band 0.05, past 0.15 m, 0.015 m of margin), the "Calm poses" joint maxima (3 per cent margin) and the floor rule (no foot below -0.02 m on any tick of a move), on every preset of the style plus KAI's body. Whatever the fitter moved is in the `ANIM_SPECS` line of the animation. The poses therefore are numerically right and **have not been looked at**: some specs carry larger offsets than a hand-made pose would (`boot` hip abduction -1.0, `footSweep` base leg 0.4/0.1/-0.55). Owner device check: look at the 59 animations in the creator preview before the next playtest.
- Context rows: a builder name on a row of another height uses the same look at that height (`HEIGHT_ANIM`: `straight` on a Mid counter is the body punch, `low` on a Mid counter is the front kick), so the identity stays `counter_strike@straight`.
- Round kicks keep the pivot of the round kick when they are derived (`ROUND_KICKS`, `STRAIGHT_KICKS` read the base builder); `techFor` hands a derived animation the style's technique of its base builder.
- Hit check test: the row count dropped from 594 to 500 pairs (Boxing has no Low rows and the grapplers no High ones, so their tables repeat rows); its floor is 480. Technique nudges needed by the stance retune and the new tables: Karate hikite elbow 0.75 > 0.65, Muay Thai off-hand 0.7 > 0.64, Sambo punch lean 0.15 > 0.05, Wrestling punch drop 0 > 0.15 and lean 0.1 > 0.25, Kali punch drop 0 > 0.1, Kickboxing and MMA `teep` leg +0.15, Kickboxing guard width 0.01 > 0 (the clothes test "hem cuts the thigh" failed at 0.01; the guard distance is 0.397 / 0.150 with it, from 0.399 / 0.150).
- Sway pose test (P3-11): a style with no strike of a height (Boxing: no Low strike; the grapplers: no High one) has nothing to check there; the longest strike of a height is now a different row for some styles, and 17 more named floors were measured (`shortSlips`: Judo collar pull and low sleeve pull, Wrestling underhook smash, Karate Kyokushin round kick, Boxing body jab, Brawler big right, Capoeira, Muay Thai and Sambo heavy round kicks, BJJ jab, Hapkido cross, Kali jab, Krav Maga jab, MMA cross and sweep), each as the measured clearance minus 0.01.
