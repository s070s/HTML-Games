# Unreal Tournament 3 (2007)

Source tags [Sn] refer to the list at the end. "unverified" = memory or snippet only.

## Identity
- Epic Games, Midway publisher. Unreal Engine 3. PC Nov 2007, PS3 Dec 2007, Xbox 360 July 2008. Announced as UT2007, delayed, renamed UT3 in Jan 2007. [S1]
- Reviews: about 83% PC, 86% PS3, 83% Xbox 360 (GameRankings); over one million copies by March 2008. [S1]
- Linux/macOS ports cancelled; PS3 online ended 2014; PC online servers shut down Jan 2023; "UT3 X" free-to-play leaked 2022, cancelled 2023. [S1]
- Eight modes at launch, with Invasion, Onslaught, Bombing Run, Last Man Standing, Domination and Double Domination dropped; Assault cut during production. [S1]

## Core loop
- Duel (1v1 with a winner-stays queue, about 15 minutes), DM, TDM, CTF, Vehicle CTF, Warfare, later Greed and Betrayal. [S1]
- Single player: a five-act story campaign replaced the ladder; same missions as multiplayer; bots replace opponents; cards give map advantages; co-op for four. Leaders unlocked by defeating them. [S2]
- Defaults for frag/time/goal limits not retrieved (unverified).

## Movement
- Gravity increased vs UT2004 and dodge-jump removed to make play less floaty. Jump Boots restore dodge-jump. [S2]
- Double jump, dodge, wall dodge remain (details unverified). No fetched numbers for run speed.
- Translocator: 7 charges, about 1 charge per second regeneration, 4.4 shots/s, lower and faster arc than UT2004. [S8]
- Hoverboard (Vehicle CTF, Warfare, Greed maps): replaces the translocator slot; faster than running; magnetic grapple tows behind friendly vehicles; no weapons while riding; flag or Orb can be carried; any hit knocks the rider off for several seconds (Shield Belt prevents falls until depleted); poor on water and ice. [S1][S10]
- Impact Hammer and Shield Belt-style jump combos (unverified).

## Health and damage
- Damage values differ for player/vehicle/node (see table). Headshot doubles sniper damage. Hoverboard rider fall on any damage. [S3][S10]
- Self-damage, fall damage and telefrag details: unverified.

## Modes and match options
- Deathmatch and TDM; CTF; Duel; Warfare (evolved Onslaught: node chain to enemy core; Orb capture, special node types); Vehicle CTF; Betrayal (shared pot, 30 s retaliation window); Greed (skulls delivered to enemy base). [S1][S15]
- Warfare: Orb spawns in each base; carrier instantly captures any capturable node, heals and shields held nodes; if the carrier dies, the Orb drops with an 18 s countdown; enemy destroying the dropped Orb loses 100 health; carrier can't use vehicles/teleporters without dropping. Node types: countdown (60 s timer, 20% core damage), vehicle, bridge, damage, core-exposing, support. [S15]
- Mutators: Big Head, Friendly Fire (half damage), Handicap, Instagib, Kills Slow Time (Instant Action), LowGrav, No Hoverboard, No Orb, No Super Pickups, No Translocator, SloMo, Speed Freak, Super Berserk, Survival (Duel), Titan, Weapon Replacement, Weapons Respawn. [S16]
- Web Admin arrived with patch 2; Linux dedicated server Dec 2007; Titan Pack with patch 4 (2.0). [S2]

## Vehicles
- Axon: Scorpion, Hellbender, Manta, Raptor, Cicada, Paladin, Hellfire SPMA, Goliath, Leviathan, Stealthbender. Necris: Viper, Darkwalker, Nemesis, Fury, Nightshade, Scavenger. Others: Hoverboard, rail turrets, Eradicator. [S2]
- Axon vehicles carry over from UT2004 with gameplay changes. [S1]

## Map design
- Large vehicle maps (WAR-*), compact DM/Duel maps (e.g., DM-Fearless with Invulnerability item respawn 109 s). [S17]
- Strong use of verticality and powerup placement (Shield Belt and UDamage near each other on some maps). [S17]

## Bots and AI
- Same skill names (Novice to Godlike), achievements reference "Godlike" and "Adept or higher". [S2]
- Voice commands for bots worked poorly and reportedly best with North American accents. [S2]
- Patch 2.0 (March 2009) and Titan Pack improved AI, especially in vehicle modes. [S1]
- Reviewers cited bots as a weakness (GamesRadar negatives: server browser, campaign, bots). [S18]
- Detailed UT3 bot parameters: not found; assume the Bot wiki table (speed %, FOV, turn rate) as carried across UT games. [S19]

## Progression
- Five-act campaign, leaders as bosses, cards as modifiers, Insane difficulty, achievements (patch 2.0). [S2]

## HUD and options
- Console-like menu; patch 2.0 added reworked UI and options; INI settings often failed to save before 2.0. [S2]
- Voice commands, announcer calls (unverified). FOV and keybinds in menus.

## Best features to keep
- Dedicated Duel mode with a queue for spectators. [S1]
- Warfare Orb idea: a single object that creates a tactical spike. [S15]
- Hoverboard for traversal on big maps without vehicles; counterplay by knock-off. [S10]
- Per-target damage values (players / vehicles / nodes) keeping objective modes balanced. [S3]
- Stronger weapon feel: increased gravity and no dodge-jump gave tighter movement. [S2]
- Handicap and Survival mutators are good for mixed-skill offline play. [S16]

## Mistakes to avoid
- Console-style UI: extra clicks, game does not remember last mode, no favorites in server browser at launch, many settings not saving. [S2][S18]
- Rushed launch: server browser "barely functioned" (forum); bots disliked; weak campaign; PS3 sluggish controls until adjusted. [S18]
- Scaled poorly with settings (GameRevolution) - relevant for low-end targets. [S18]
- Dropped popular modes (Invasion, Bombing Run, LMS, Domination, Assault) which angered fans; the community scene fragmented. [S1]
- Modding harder because scripting moved to native code. [S2]

## Adaptation notes
- Keep Duel, DM, TDM, CTF, plus a simplified Warfare (3 nodes + Orb) only if time allows.
- Use per-victim damage multipliers rather than separate weapons for any vehicles/objectives.
- Hoverboard = fast ground-glide with knock-off on any hit; cheap to implement as a movement state.
- UI: one-screen quick match with remembered options, no INI-style settings; avoid the issues above.
- Use the cut-down weapon list: Shock, Link, Stinger-like gun, Flak, Rocket, Sniper, Bio, Redeemer.
