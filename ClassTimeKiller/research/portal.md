# Portal (with Portal 2 notes)

Tags [n] refer to Sources. "unverified" = author recollection or not on a fetched page. "snippet" = seen only in a search excerpt.

## Identity
- First-person puzzle-platformer; player is Chell using a portal gun to link blue and orange portals; sparse test-chamber settings under GLaDOS [1].
- Grew from DigiPen student project Narbacular Drop; reused HL2 assets; sparse look chosen partly because testers were distracted by decor [1][17].
- Metacritic 90 (PC) [1]; Portal 2 Metacritic 95 [6]. Criticized for short length and sparse environments [1].
- Team grew from 7-8 people (Portal) to 30-40 (Portal 2) [6].

## Core loop
- Sequence of chambers; each introduces one new idea, early ones with heavy hints that fade; flinging taught without explicit text [1][17].
- Valve approach: extended training exercise, tools introduced one at a time then layered into harder puzzles; buttons and boxes taught before portals [17].
- Chambers often have several solutions [1]; Valve accepted some skip solutions when they cost more skill than the intended route [17].
- After main game: Challenge Chambers (fewest portals, fewest steps, least time) and Advanced Chambers [3][20].
- Portal 2 development: dropped-and-restored portals after testers disliked the replacement "F-Stop" idea (redirected about October 2008); lasers replaced energy balls for faster feedback; a third gel was cut because it made testers sick; competitive co-op lines tested poorly; chambers solved too easily were sent back for redesign [6].
- Chamber types in Portal 2: "checklisting" practice rooms and lateral-thinking puzzles; aim is to make players feel clever, not to raise difficulty [6].

## Movement
- Momentum is conserved through portals: falling into a floor portal and exiting a wall portal flings the player [1][6]. Exit speed numbers and any speed cap: unverified.
- Valve found momentum the hardest concept to convey: introduced slowly, with a visual cue (a pushed-out slab over a checkerboard pit) repeated until associated with the fling; the double fling got its own simplified room and the last chamber removes hints [17].
- No sprint; fall damage absent (unverified; player has long-fall boots in Portal 2 story).
- Aerial Faith Plate (Portal 2): launches the player or objects over gaps at a set velocity and height; keeps speed through portals; standard launch directions are 3 (up, forward, backward) with one sideways exception late; the education version lists impulse 1 to 99 N, angle 0 to 90 degrees [10].
- Portal 2 gels: orange propulsion reduces friction and boosts speed; blue repulsion bounces (jump height tracks fall height, can ricochet between parallel surfaces); white conversion makes a surface portalable; one gel per surface, newest gel wins, water removes gel; no gel on glass, grilles pass through [12][6].
- Excursion funnel (tractor beam): players and objects inside become weightless and flow along the beam; can be reversed by a button, passes through portals, carries gel along [16]. Hard Light Bridge passes through portals and can be gelled temporarily [13].

## Abilities and items
- Co-op (Portal 2 only, noted): two robots with a gun each; ping, countdown, gestures [6]; Valve considers co-op much harder to design than single-player [6].
- Repulsion gel makes turrets bounce; propulsion makes them slide so they topple more easily [22].
- Portal gun achievements/uses beyond the puzzle set: unverified.

## Health and damage
- Chell has regenerating health (unverified exact); instant death from goo and pellets [1][7].
- Turret damage per bullet not listed on page; a few seconds of sustained fire kills a human; robots Atlas and P-body last longer [22].
- Laser beam: sizzle, push back, small damage, lethal only with prolonged exposure [14].
- Early prototype electrified rails were replaced by light beams after one-hit kills frustrated testers [17].

## Modes and match options
- Challenge mode: bronze, silver, gold medals for fewest portals, fewest steps, least time; Test Chambers 13 to 18 each carry three challenges; all-bronze, all-silver, all-gold unlock one achievement each (Basic Science, Rocket Science, Aperture Science) [3][20][21].
- Example thresholds (snippet): Chamber 13 steps bronze 30, silver 20, gold 10; Chamber 14 portal par about 2 [20][21].
- Challenge goals tighten as you match them; Portal 2 adds Steam leaderboards [3].
- Advanced chambers: six remixed versions of chambers 13 to 18, unlocked from the start, with fewer portalable surfaces or hazards added [3][20].
- Portal 2 Peer Review DLC (October 2011) added co-op campaign [6].
- Clone modes: portal-par challenge maps (par portals, par steps, par time), medal ladder. Valve tried multiplayer in Portal 1 and found portals unfun in deathmatch; a Portal 2 competitive mode was dropped [18][6].

## Map design
- One concept per room, unambiguous visuals, minimal decoration; early cluttered HL2-style environments were simplified [1][17].
- Starting room has distinctive objects so portals read as links between nearby spaces; first portal is placed so you see yourself through it [17].
- Gates: a forced pause makes you notice the gun firing; a puzzle needing at least five portals in order prevents blundering; a room retrains which surfaces accept portals [17].
- Playtesting from the first week of production [9 snippet]; observers watch in person, and testers are easy to recruit from friends, family and staff [18].
- Cues: ladder to make players look up, pistons to show exit portals, ticking sound with pedestal button, slow lifts instead of stairs to push players toward portal routes [17].
- Decorative catwalks became real paths because testers kept trying to reach them [17].
- Fizzlers and elevators mark chamber ends so players cannot portal across level loads [17].
- Early weighted button on Test Chamber 11 was replaced by a pedestal button after testers searched for a cube [15].

## Bots and AI
- Turret perception: line of sight only, glass blocks shooting (tracks but does not fire through one pane; may fire through two), hard light bridge blocks detection, 3 s search then sleep [4][22].
- Turret behaviour was designed as a character: cute voice contrasting lethal function; rockets and speech were tried and removed [17].
- Turret kill methods: knock over, portal under feet, pellet, fizzler, laser; gel affects bounce and slide [22].
- Bot idea: "turret mode" bots with a vision cone, laser sweep, fall-over state when picked up.

## Progression
- Linear chambers 00-19 plus challenges (count unverified); no stats. Portal 1 gold medals tied to achievements [21].

## HUD and options
- Minimal crosshair showing portal states (filled when placed). Subtitles/captions exist (unverified).

## Best features to keep
- Tool rules small enough to teach in one room; introduce, reinforce with rising challenge, then combine [1][19].
- Challenge medals with par values [3].
- Energy ball/cube/button vocabulary; faith plate, funnel and gel as modular add-ons [10][12][16].
- Momentum preservation as a mobility system.
- Giving the held box a personality and dialogue so players keep it [18].

## Mistakes to avoid
- Short length and sparse environments [1]; Portal 2 chatter weakened mood per one critic (Ars Technica) [6].
- Testers got sick from adhesion/third gel [6][12]; avoid disorienting portal view jitter.
- Box marathon level frustrated testers; boss ideas (lasers, "Portal Kombat", chase) failed on pacing and fun [18].
- Too many concepts in the first energy-ball puzzle, so two chambers were added before it [17].
- Testers leapt blindly through portals, so a "look first" puzzle was added [17].
- Co-op not needed for offline bot game (mention only).

## Adaptation notes
- Portal rendering cost: each portal needs a second view render; on low-end phones limit to 1 pair with render-to-texture at 256x256-512x512 and stencil/clip plane; or skip view and use a "warp pad" teleport with momentum transfer (flash cut). (design suggestion)
- Collision: portals only on flat tagged wall quads, axis-aligned; teleport player by matrix transform of position and velocity.
- Rules to copy: two portals max, same-colour replaces, fizzler wipes portals, gel-tagged surfaces become portalable.
- Bot portal use: precompute portal pairs and let bots path through them via nav links.
- Challenge mode in bot match: "portal arena" with par scores.
