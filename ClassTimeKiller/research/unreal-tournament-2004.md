# Unreal Tournament 2004 (UT2004)

Source tags [Sn] refer to the list at the end. "unverified" = memory or snippet only.

## Identity
- Epic Games / Digital Extremes, 2004, Unreal Engine 2 (Karma physics). Updated re-release of UT2003 adding vehicles, Onslaught, Assault and voice. [S1][S2]
- Metacritic 93, GameRankings 94%; highest-rated Unreal-series game. Over 100 maps (121 official with bonus packs), 90 characters, optional third-person camera. [S1][S2]
- Fans disliked "floatier" movement and more dodging that made projectile hits on skilled players harder. [S2]

## Core loop
- Fast arena DM with adrenaline combos; larger vehicle modes for objective play. [S1]
- Ladder-based single player (DM, TDM, CTF, Double Domination, Bombing Run, Onslaught, Assault) with a final challenge against Xan; Godlike needed to unlock Xan the hard way (forum). Exact ladder rules not retrieved. [S12]
- Default limits (frag, time, overtime): not found (unverified). Onslaught has sudden-death overtime where cores drain; core kill 2 points regulation, 1 in overtime. [S10]

## Movement
- Double jump, dodge, dodge-jump, wall dodge, shield-gun jumps. Boost dodge was an accident in UT2003 and removed in 2004. [S1][S6][S7]
- Measured distances in UU (default gravity, no combo): jump 54 high / 315 far; dodge 19-20 high / 292-351 far; jump+double jump 126 / 608; dodge+double jump 90 / 742-857; jump, double jump, wall dodge 149 / 516-965. [S6]
- Speed combo roughly doubles jump heights (jump 130 / 675; jump+DJ 279 / 1228). Shield-gun impact jump up to 455 height straight down. [S6]
- Run speed and gravity values: unverified (not on the metrics page).
- Dodge-jump and wall dodge were introduced in UT2003. [S7]
- Translocator: 6 charges (UT2003 5), slow regeneration. [S8]
- Impact jumping with the Shield Gun, Hoverboard absent (UT3 only).

- Shield stacks: small stack 50, large stack 150, total cap 150; absorbs 75% at or under 100, 100% above (simplified). [S14][S15]
- Adrenaline: 100 needed. Gains: pill +2, DM frag +5, CTF/BR/DDOM frag +2, first blood/spree/end spree/multikill +10 each, kill flag carrier +10, return flag +5, capture +25, suicide -2. Absent in Last Man Standing, Onslaught, Assault. [S16]
- Combos (key sequence): Speed (Up x4, about 9-18 s), Booster (Down x4, regenerates health to 199% then shield to 150%, about 4-5/s), Invisibility (Right Right Left Left, 27 s), Berserk (Up Up Down Down, 27 s, faster fire and more knockback); bonus-pack Pint-sized and Camouflaged. [S16]

## Health and damage
- Start 100 health; normal cap 100, super cap 199; shield cap 150. [S15]
- Headshots double Lightning/Sniper damage. Shock combo 200. [S3][S9]
- Vehicle damage is separate from player damage for most weapons (see table). [S3]
- Self damage values, fall damage, telefrag: unverified (telefrag via translocator is documented). [S8]

## Modes and match options
- Deathmatch, Team DM, Capture the Flag, Vehicle CTF, Onslaught, Assault, Double Domination, Bombing Run, Last Man Standing, Invasion (wave survival against Unreal monsters, no respawns), Mutant (first kill becomes the mutant; powers pass on kill). [S1][S2]
- Double Domination: hold both control points for ten seconds to score. [S1]
- Bombing Run: running the ball through the hoop = 7; shooting it through = 3; ball resets after 15 s untouched; carrier uses Ball Launcher and cannot shoot. [S17]
- Onslaught: link power nodes (usually 2000 HP) to the enemy core (5000 HP on stock maps, ONS-Dria 9000); cores can't be healed; Link Gun alt-fire speeds node building. [S10]
- Assault: ordered objectives; types Carry, Destroy, Timed presence, Carry key, Use key; carried items reset after 30 s. [S18]
- Mutators (many): Air Control, Arena, BigHead, Big Wheels, Bonus Combos, Bonus Vehicles, Game Speed, InstaGib, Lightning Guns, Lightweight Vehicles, LowGrav, No Adrenaline, No Superweapons, Onslaught Weapons, QuadJump, Regeneration, Slow Motion Corpses, Sniper Rifles, Species Statistics, Stunt Vehicles, Super Berserk, UDamage Reward, UT Classic, UT2003 Style, Vampire, Vehicle Arena, Zoom InstaGib. [S11]

## Map design
- Prefixes DM, TDM, CTF, VCTF, ONS, AS, BR, DOM, INV. Onslaught maps use a graph of nodes with garages for vehicles; vehicle spawn varies per node (e.g., Leviathans only at certain nodes). [S20]
- DM maps compact with item spawns for UDamage, Super Shield, Keg (e.g., DM-Deck17 remake). [S21]

## Bots and AI
- Same 8 levels (Novice to Godlike). Detailed per-level values as listed by the wiki (UT2004-based): Novice 60% speed, no movement in combat, 30° FOV, aim off ±30°, turn under 180°/s; Average 70%; Experienced 80% + translocator + strafes + 40° FOV; Skilled 90% + double jump + 60° FOV + 225°/s; Adept full speed, dodges incoming fire, closes distance, leads aim for projectiles, 80° FOV; Masterful uses Impact Hammer/Shield Gun jump, shock combos, switches targets, repairs nodes before leaving in Onslaught, 100° FOV, 315°/s; Inhuman tries to dodge aim, 120° FOV, bails from vehicle when an AVRiL would kill it; Godlike aim within 1°, 360° FOV, turns in under 0.5 s. [S22]
- Each bot character has weapon preferences. Bots handle voice commands and Onslaught AI. Orders: attack, defend, freelance, hold (via squad system). [S1][S22]
- Squad system: help requests create squads; followers detour then return; don't conflict with leader. [S22]
- Bots treat spawn points and item pickups as safe; get stuck on bad pathing; react instantly to projectiles. [S22]
- Fairness: superhuman aim with flak, minigun, link at close range, but predictable; a moderate human can beat Godlike 1v1. [S22]
- UT2004 served as the platform for the BotPrize Turing test. [S1]
- Personality attributes in config (accuracy, combat style, strafing ability, alertness, camping, jumpy, favorite weapon) inherited from UT99 design (unverified for 2004).

## Progression
- Ladder with per-gametype tiers; Xan Kriegor as final; unlocks characters. Exact structure not retrieved. [S12]
- Adrenaline resets each life.

## HUD and options
- Voice commands for bots, VoIP, announcer medals (Killing Spree, Rampage, Dominating, Unstoppable, Godlike; Double/Multi/Mega/Ultra/Monster Kill) (known; no fetched source). Minimal HUD with adrenaline bar.
- FOV, crosshair, weapon-priority list, optional third-person view. [S1]

## Best features to keep
- Adrenaline combos: skill-reward system with visible meter. [S16]
- Link Gun as support weapon (heal and boost). [S5]
- Vehicles with clear roles and simple counters (AVRiL). [S19]
- Node-chain objectives (Onslaught) as a cheap capture-point graph for bots. [S10]
- Rich mutator list, trivially supported by rule flags. [S11]
- Bot skill table with explicit FOV, speed, turn rate numbers - a ready recipe. [S22]

## Mistakes to avoid
- Floaty movement and excess dodging frustrated hitscan-vs-projectile balance. [S2]
- Godlike/Inhuman bots cheat (360° vision, instant reactions). [S22]
- Netcode break with UT2003 shows the cost of rebuilding; plan stable save/format. [S2]
- Online scene shrank to Onslaught, TAM/Freon, Invasion by 2009 (mode fragmentation). [S2]
- Vehicle bots can get stuck on bad path data. [S22]

## Adaptation notes
- Drop vehicles except a simple hover-bike/turret for low-end performance; keep Manta-style single-seater as optional.
- Adrenaline combos as 4 key taps on desktop; on touch, four on-screen buttons shown only at 100.
- Use dodge double-tap or a dedicated dash button for touch; double jump as second tap.
- Map the bot skill table onto parameters: speed %, FOV, turn rate, aim error. Keep skill-adjusting off or limited to avoid cheating feel.
- Modes cheap for bots: DM, TDM, CTF, DOM, DDOM, LMS, Mutant, Invasion; Onslaught reduced to a 3-node chain.
