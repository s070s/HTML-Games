# PvE (S14 14h..14k) - enemies, director, PvE modes and the boss

Files: `src/03o-data-enemies.js` (enemy rows E01..E14 and range targets R01..R03, director, champion, wave, mode and chamber tables), `src/14h-pve.js` (framework `Pve`: spawning, perception, brains, attack phases, director, damage hooks, drawing), `src/14i-pve-enemies.js` (`PveMove`, `PveAttacks`, `PveBrains`: one generic function set per behaviour and attack kind), `src/14j-pve-modes.js` (mode behaviours, `PVE_HUD`), `src/14k-pve-boss.js` (`PveBoss`, E13 phases). Tests: `src/20b-test-pve.js` (group `pve`), `src/20b-test-simwire.js` (group `simwire`: sliders, events, level report, bank on Cistern). Debug: `__CTK.pve` (`pve`, `boss`, `hud`, `showcase(mapId?)` lines every row up in front of a player, `chamberRoutes()` prints each MP11 chamber's walked route against its par). Spec: NPC-001..NPC-008, BOT-005, DIF-002, DIF-004, PERF-009, MAP-030, M21..M30, X50.

## Contract

- Enemies are `ACTOR_KIND.ENEMY` actors with `Actors.profile` = their `CAT.enemies` row. Only `Pve.spawnEnemy` makes them (spawning contract: placed, `alive = 1`, `actor.spawn` emitted, then the row's pools, size, head size and movement preset override what S11 / S10 / S13h set). They are never `Modes.participant`; kills by participants credit the participant like any kill.
- They reuse the bot layers: view cone and line-of-sight ray (`BotSense.viewClass`, `BotSense.lineOpen`, memory arrays), path following (`BotMove`), and the motor (`Bots.motor`). They never run the bot brain (`Bots.active` stays 0), never call `BotSense.look` (no team pings or barks) and spend their own ray budget (`PVE_DIRECTOR.raysPerTick`, 24 per tick).
- Sim systems: `pveBrains` (order 11: think at 4 Hz staggered, attack phases, movement, cloak, motor), `pve` (order 80: director queue, corpses, champions, X50 wildlife), `pveBoss` (order 81: boss HUD).
- Enemy actors are hidden from the humanoid renderer (`Render.addActorStyle`) and drawn by the `pve` render source from the row `parts` (instanced shapes of the props batch), plus a ring in the champion tag colour under champions and rings in the element colour (`CAT.elements` colour; plain shields 0xa0d0ff) around shielded enemies while `Actors.shield > 0`. The `pveChambers` source draws the puzzle props. Every `pve.tell` also plays a positional cue (`Sound.play`: green `abilityCast`, red `pingDanger`, orange `grenadeTick`).
- Hit volumes are the S08 upright stack scaled by `row.size` (`Hitbox.sizeScale`), the head sphere also by `row.headScale` (`Hitbox.headScale`, times the X14 mutator). Silhouettes sit on those volumes: every `weak` and `body` part centre lies inside a volume, every other part centre within 0.5 m of one, and no `body` part reaches more than 0.3 m past the torso radius (self-tests). The boss's glowing `weak` head is its head sphere.
- Per step no allocation: events reuse payload objects (copy what you keep), the damage infos come from the S11 pool, spawn options are reused objects.

## Enemy rows (`CAT.enemies`)

`{ id, code, name, role, brain, health, speed, shield, shieldElement, cruise, size, headScale, fodder, heavy, leader, beast, boss, target, cash, cost, sight, fov, turn, reaction, keepRange: [min, max], massFactor, flees, takesCover, cloaked, blink, heal, weakPoint, frontShield, group, drop, dropChance, meleeDrop, phases, endlessFrom, attacks, answers, parts, color, glow }`

- `attacks`: `{ id, kind, damage, range: [min, max], tell (s, >= 0.4), cooldown, recover, parry (green tell), unblockable (red tell), shots, gap, spreadDeg, radius, weapon (projectile attacks: a weapon row), splashScale, fanDeg, element, seconds, knockdown, summon, limit, searchSeconds }`; row order = priority. Defaults: `ENEMY_ATTACK_DEFAULTS` (14h).
- Attack kinds (`PveAttacks`): `melee` (one blow in reach and in front; parryable ones fail inside the parry window), `burst` (hitscan shots `gap` apart), `laser` (one exact shot, aim locks 0.3 s before; red beam during the tell), `projectile` (the weapon row's projectile, speed x `projectileSpeed`; `fanDeg` spreads `shots`; with `parry: true` its bolts fly back from a target that pressed MELEE or ALT inside the parry window, with any weapon), `slam` (ground shockwave: jump it), `explode` (bomber blast, also on death if not yet blown), `charge` (locked rush, knockdown on contact; a wall stuns the charger), `pounce` (ballistic leap), `summon` (rows of `summon`, a swarm row brings its whole group, at most `limit` alive).
- Behaviour kinds (`PveBrains`): `melee ranged heavy shielded sniper bomber beast leaper charger` (generic priority brain), `stalker` (cloaked while idle; visible for its whole attack - tell, blow, recovery - and around its blink, which a 0.4 s shimmer announces; shock reveals it), `support` (heals its team in `heal.radius`), `turret` (static; watches the last known place `searchSeconds`, then sweeps), `dummy` (range targets), `boss` (14k). Hooks (all optional): `always(id, row, now)` (every tick, even while stunned), `decide`, `step`, `move`, `allows`, `reduce`, `draw`.
- `answers`: at least two counters per enemy (shown by UI / codex; the self-test checks the count).
- `parts`: `[shape, x, y, z, sx, sy, sz, tint, aim]` metres at size 1; tint `body | dark | glow | weak | eye`; `aim` 1 = the part pitches with the aim.
- `weakPoint.kind`: `back` (shots travelling along the facing x mult), `air` (x mult while airborne), `core` (boss head: x mult, drains `pool`; an empty pool disables the phase's signature attack for `seconds`). `frontShield`: hits from inside `arcDeg` x `factor`, except while attacking and `lowered` s after.

## Brain (NPC-001..NPC-007)

Priority at each think: flee (broken morale, or `flees` fodder outnumbered x2 within 20 m) > dodge a foe's grenade, mine or rocket within 4 m > fall back (NPC-007: the encounter lost 75% of what it spawned while enemies are still queued; the survivors return to their spawn point and regroup; never the boss, turrets, beasts or range targets) > cover when hurt below 40% (`takesCover`) > attack (seen target past its reaction time, a free attack token - `rules.botSkill.attackers` attackers per target, the boss exempt - and an attack in range off cooldown) > lure (X50 beasts) > pursue > search (stealth) > mode objective (`Pve.objectiveFor`) > idle (stealth guards patrol their home).

- Attack phases: `WINDUP` (the tell: `pve.tell` event and sound, the body glows in the tell colour, green 0x40ff60 parryable, red 0xff2020 unblockable, orange otherwise) -> `ACTIVE` -> `RECOVER`. A stun (`ACTOR_FLAG.FROZEN`) or stagger cancels the attack.
- Parry: a parryable blow lands on nothing when its target pressed MELEE or ALT within `rules.botSkill.parry` s; the attacker staggers 1.2 s (`pve.parry`). A parryable projectile reaching a target that pressed inside the window, from its front (70 degrees), flies back owned by that target (`pve.parry` with attack `projectile`; S11 hook `WeaponSpecialState.parryHooks`).
- Stagger: `Pve.stagger(id, seconds, cause)` with `STAGGER_CAUSE` `damage` / `parry` / `wall` (no cause = forced). A hit of >= 25% of max health staggers 1.2 s. Never the boss; unstoppable champions ignore damage staggers and damage knockback (only parries and walls stagger them). Melee kills of staggered enemies drop a health pack (finisher); `meleeDrop` on other melee kills; else `drop` with `dropChance x resourceYield`.
- Morale: a `leader` dying breaks the fodder of its team within 20 m for 3..6 s. Last stand (NPC-008): nothing queued and a quarter of the peak alive: nobody flees or falls back.
- Stealth (`Pve.stealth`, M28, BOT-005): `Pve.awareness[id]` fills while the guard sees a foe (0.6 s near .. 2.5 s at view range, slower for still or crouched targets), decays after 6 s unseen; 0.3 suspicious, 0.6 searching, 1 alert (combat, `pve.alarm`, guards within 15 m alerted). Noises within 60% of their radius make a guard suspicious. A marker above the head shows the state.

## Difficulty (DIF-002, DIF-004, `rules.botSkill`)

One reader per slider. `playerDamage` (enemy damage to everybody not an enemy) and `enemyDamage` (damage to enemies) are applied once, by `Pve.attackerScale` (registered as a `Damage.addAttackerScale`; nothing else scales damage by difficulty), `attackers` (tokens), `parry` (window, s; also scales the player's W03 parry window against the 0.3 s default, `DIFFICULTY_PARRY_BASE`, in 11d), `projectileSpeed`, `resourceYield` (drop chance), `aggression` (wave budgets), `lives` (0 = unlimited; set on every participant at a PvE start), `checkpoints` and `checkpointHeal` (wave retries), `gameSpeed` (14j registers a `difficulty` rule modifier that multiplies it into `rules.fx.timeScale` for the eight PvE modes before the ruleset freezes; S13h turns that into `Sim.timeScale`; versus modes keep normal speed).

Guardian Plate (DIF-004): `Pve.guardian[id] = 1` halves enemy damage to that participant (`PVE_DIRECTOR.guardianTaken`). The wave modes offer it after 3 participant deaths in one wave (`guardianDeaths`; HUD `guardianOffer`) and put it on everybody for the next checkpoint retry, once per run; that retry costs no `retries` (it also saves a run with none left, when `checkpoints` is on). It comes off when the wave is cleared (HUD `guardian`).

## Director (NPC-007, NPC-008, PERF-009)

- `Pve.enqueue(key, side, scale, champion, wave)` queues a spawn; `Pve.serveQueue` spawns at most 2 per tick, a whole `group` (swarm) at once, only while `aliveCount + group <= Pve.cap()` (= `Quality.current().enemies`: 40 / 32 / 24 / 18 / 12), and not for 8 s after an intensity peak (damage taken by participants). An entry whose first member finds no free actor slot (corpses hold slots 3 s) stays queued. Points: `Pve.spawnPoints` `{ x, y, z, yaw, side }` filled by the mode; the best is >= 18 m from the hunted players and out of their sight; a side without points uses a reachable nav node away from the players.
- `side`: -1 the co-op enemy side; 0 / 1 a team's PvE (M23) or minions (M29). `Pve.sideTeam(side)` / `Pve.sideHunt(side)` (set by the mode) give the spawned team and the hunted team.
- Encounters: the wave modes call `Pve.beginEncounter()` at each wave start; `Pve.encounterSpawned` counts its spawns for the fallback share (`PVE_DIRECTOR.fallbackLosses` 0.75, from `fallbackMinimum` 4 spawns on).
- `scale` multiplies health and damage (endless). Champions (`PVE_CHAMPIONS`, a ring in their `glow` under them): barrier (shield, regenerates unless stunned; 30% carry an element: x1.5 for that element, x0.75 for others), unstoppable (x1.3 health, no damage stagger, no knockback), overload (health regenerates unless shocked).
- Corpses are removed 3 s after death (actor slots are freed).
- X50 Wildlife: `rules.fx.wildlife` E14 beasts (team -1, hostile to all, preferring whoever hurt them) on random reachable nodes >= 18 m from participants; killed beasts return after 30 s; explosions of `flash` / `incendiary` gadgets or `thermal` element lure beasts within 30 m for 6 s.

## API (`Pve`)

| Call | Does |
|---|---|
| `spawnEnemy(keyOrRow, x, y, z, yaw, { team, side, huntTeam, scale, champion, wave, summoner })` | id or -1 (cap, no slot, unknown key) |
| `spawnGroup(row, x, y, z, yaw, options)` | the row's whole group in a spiral; returns how many spawned (0 when the first member failed) |

| `clearEnemies(filter?)` | frees enemy slots (and the queue when no filter) |
| `aliveCount(side?)` / `hostileCount(side?)` / `countBeasts()` | living enemies / without range targets and beasts / beasts |
| `beginEncounter()` / `shouldFallBack(row)` / `lastStand()` | NPC-007 / NPC-008 director states |
| `isEnemy(id)` / `rowOf(id)` / `cap()` / `skill()` | |
| `setCore(x, y, z, health)` / `damageCore(amount, source)` / `core` | the M21 / M22 core (`{ active, x, y, z, health, max, shield }`) |
| `startAttack(id, index, target, now)` / `cancelAttack(id)` / `stagger(id, seconds, cause?)` | |
| `parryProjectile(target, projectile)` | the S11 parry hook (true = reflect it) |
| `awareness[id]`, `awarenessState(id)` | stealth meter 0..1, `AWARENESS` |
| `relocateActor(id, x, y, z, yaw)` (global) | moves an actor with no interpolation and no velocity (spawns, blinks, chambers) |

Events: `pve.tell { id, attack, kind, seconds, color, target }`, `pve.parry { id, attack, kind, target }`, `pve.alarm { id, target }`, `pve.core { id, kind: 'destroyed' }`, `pve.wave { wave, phase: 'start'|'end' }` (end = cleared; a lost wave that restarts sends no end), `pve.boss { id, phase, phases, kind: 'phase'|'weak'|'defeated', seconds (since the boss spawned), final }` (`final`: a defeated boss that ends the level, from the mode behaviour's `finalBoss(id)`: the last horde wave's boss, either bank boss), `pve.chamber { chamber, kind: 'enter'|'complete', medal, stars, seconds }`, `pve.secret { id, secret }`, `pve.crate { id, prop }`. Payloads are reused. S15 reads them (docs/api/progression.md).

## Level report (14j `PveLevel`)

- Secrets: the map's `secret` nav hints (at most 16). A participant within 1.5 m finds one: `pve.secret`, the announcement "Secret found". Cistern has 2, Ash Manor 3.
- Crates: the map's breakable `props` (S08 `Props`; only those within the quality budget break). A participant breaking one (`world.break` with `source`) sends `pve.crate`. Cistern and Ash Manor have 3 each.
- Optional enemies: contract guards (`PveLevel.markOptional`) and X50 beasts.
- `PveLevel.report(match)` on `match.end` writes `match.results.pve = { enemiesLeft, propsLeft, secretsLeft, optionalLeft }` with only the numbers the level can measure (see docs/api/progression.md).

## Boss (E13, 14k)

Phases from `row.phases` (`until` = health share): 1 slam; 2 barrage (green, parryable plasma fan of 7 with the middle bolt at the target) + slam; 3 summons (two E01 and one E07 swarm of 8) + barrage + slam. A hit never takes the boss below its current phase's threshold (`PveBrains.boss.reduce` clamps it); reaching it cancels the attack and makes the boss invulnerable for 2 s (`BOSS_TRANSITION_SECONDS`), one phase at a time, checked every tick even while frozen or staggered (`always` hook). The head is the core weak point: the glowing `weak` head part is the head hit sphere (size 2.2, `headScale` 1.6: radius 0.49 m at 3.65 m). `PveBoss.hud { active, id, name, health (share), phase, phases, weakDisabled }`.

## Modes (14j)

| Mode | Rules |
|---|---|
| M21 `horde` | Core (map objective `core`, else the central spot) with `params.coreHealth`; 10 waves from `PVE_HORDE_WAVES` (budget x aggression x players; wave 5 an unstoppable brute, wave 10 the overlord). Shop open 20 s between waves (`Economy.setShopOpen`), wave bonus by `Economy.awardWave` (flawless when no participant died); buy item `coreShield` adds 1000 core shield. Lost wave (core down or every participant out of lives): retry from the wave start while `params.retries` are left and `checkpoints` is on (core back to its wave-start health, everyone revived and healed to `checkpointHeal`), or with the Guardian Plate once; else defeat. Win after wave 10. |
| M22 `endless` | Same loop; budget 10 + 4 per wave, enemies gated by `endlessFrom`; health and damage x (1 + 0.08 x (wave - 1)) up to x4; a champion brute every 10 waves; after every 5th wave the `extract` zone opens 20 s: a participant inside wins (`extracted`). No retries. |
| M23 `bank` | Each team fights its own PvE side (hostile to all, hunting that team) spawned at its base (6 fodder kept, topped up every 0.5 s). Killing your side's enemies carries motes (fodder 1, heavy / leader 3, at most 15, lost on death); touching the team's `bank` objective banks them (team score). A deposit of `params.blockerAt[k]` (5 / 10 / 15) or more sends `PVE_BANK.blockers[k]` (gunner / phalanx / brute) to the other side; each `params.invasionAt` (50) banked sends a barrier charger (invasion); `params.bossAt` (100) banked summons the team's overlord at 25% health; the first team to kill its boss wins. Map: MP07 Cistern (team 0 the west half, team 1 the east half, banks `bankWest` / `bankEast` by the pump platforms); without a map bank the team spawn centre is used. |
| M26 `fiends` | Ravager (the player, else the first participant, team 0, `ravagerHealth` / `ravagerArmor`) vs fiends (team 1). Fiends summon fodder every 8 s (4 alive each) and stand up 20 s after falling while a partner lives; rounds end on a team wipe. |
| M28 `contract` | Stealth: guards (E02) at the map's team-1 spawns; take the `relic` objective and reach `extract`. Score = 1000 - 2 / s - 150 / alarm - 50 / kill, + 500 without alarms, + 300 without kills. Defeat when out of lives. |
| M29 `attrition` | Minions (fodder, 15% heavies) per team walk to the enemy base; points: fodder 1, heavy 3, participant 5 (row `scoring.kill`; the behaviour pays the difference for minions). Score limit 300, time 900 s. |
| M30 `range` | Free practice: the range never ends on its own. Stations on the map's `dummy` hints cycle target / strafer / fire-back with shields 0, 0, 50, 100; killed dummies return after 2 s. Stats: shots, hits (one per shot however many pellets land; splash and damage over time add damage only), headshots, damage, kills, accuracy (<= 1). Drills: every 10 kills complete a drill (score from its time, best time kept, announced) and the next one starts. |
| M31 `puzzle` | MP11 Test Chambers: one chamber at a time (`PVE_CHAMBERS`); entering one shows its `hint` (HUD `hint` and an announcement; the last chambers have none, MAP-030). `lock` none / button (USE within 1.5 m) / plate (the carried cube - USE to lift and drop - on it) / relay (a shot passing within 0.6 m) / all; a fizzle grid returns a carried cube, and one with `clears: 'gates'` closes the crosser's twin gates (W42). The exit objective `exitN` completes the chamber when unlocked; medal = the best of gold / silver / bronze whose time, footstep and portal (W42 shots) limits all hold (3 / 2 / 1 stars). The 12th exit wins. Self-test: every exit, button, cube, plate and relay firing spot is reachable on the nav graph from its chamber spawn, and the walked route fits the gold time and silver footstep par. |
