# Weapons, projectiles and damage (S11) - public API

Files: `src/03c-data-weapons.js`, `src/03d-data-health.js` (data), `src/11a-weapons.js`, `src/11b-projectiles.js`, `src/11c-damage.js`, `src/11d-weapons-special.js`, `src/11e-weapons-muzzle.js` (code), `src/03b-data-animations.js` and `src/07g-render-weapon-anim.js` (the P6 reload and melee animations, docs/api/render.md), `src/20b-test-anims.js` (group `anims`), `src/20b-test-damage.js`, `src/20b-test-weapons.js`, `src/20b-test-muzzle.js` (self-test groups `damage`, `weapons` and `muzzle`).

## Sim systems

| Order | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | `status` | `Damage.step`: status effects (DoT ticks, stuns), regeneration, overheal decay, downed bleed-out |
| 40 | `weapons` | `Weapons.step`: per-actor weapon state machine, firing, hitscan, melee, cones |
| 45 | `weaponSpecials` | held objects of the Graviton Lifter, twin-gate teleports |
| 50 | `projectiles` | `Projectiles.step`: pooled projectiles swept against the world and actor hit volumes |

Every system runs without a match (`DEFAULT_RULES` = spec defaults) and without renderer, audio or DOM.

## Ruleset fields read (`Game.match.rules`)

`health` (HPP key, default `stack`), `precision` (`arena`/`standard`/`tactical`/`hero`), `weaponSet` (default `arenaSet`), `friendlyFire` (0), `selfDamage` (0.5), `knockback` (1; applied once, by S10, to every `impulse`: `Damage.knockback` does not multiply it again). Optional: `carry` (number, overrides the weapon set's carry limit), `ammoTiers` (boolean, overrides the set's).

## Input read

`Actors.buttons` / `Actors.pressed`: `FIRE` (primary), `ALT` (alternate fire, aim, block, toggle, detonate), `RELOAD`, `MELEE` (quick melee: the carried melee weapon's primary, else a 30 damage bash), `NEXT_WEAPON`, `PREV_WEAPON`; `Actors.slotRequest` (1-9, acted on when it changes; repeated presses cycle weapons sharing a slot). Shots fly along `Actors.yaw` / `Actors.pitch` (the view the movement section sets from `aimYaw`/`aimPitch`) plus the recoil punch.

Firing is blocked while `ACTOR_FLAG.FROZEN` or `ACTOR_FLAG.DOWNED` is set.

## Data catalogues

- `CAT.weaponClasses`: `id slot tier ('primary'|'special'|'heavy'|'none') precision pistol firstShotExact ttk [min,max] equipTime reload ammoType color reloadAnim meleeAnim`. Class values are the defaults for weapon rows (`reloadAnim` = the reload set R1..R14 of `CAT.reloadAnims`, `meleeAnim` = the quick melee bash of `CAT.meleeAnims`).
- `CAT.weapons` (W01..W46 without the retired W08, W11, W14, W16, W18, W22, W29 and W33: 38 rows; the retired keys and codes map through `WEAPON_MIGRATION`, 04-save): spec Weapon schema plus `code`, `reload` (s, per shell when `reloadShell`), `altAmmo` (separate alt rounds), `dualWield` (W04: a second pickup makes a pair), `reloadAnim` / `meleeAnim` / `meleeAnimAlt` (the animation rows of the weapon when they differ from its class: P6, visual only), derived `reloadAnimIndex` (-1 = the weapon never reloads: W01, W02, W03, W41, W42, W44), `meleeAnimIndex`, `meleeAltIndex`, derived `index` (typed-array index), `family` (class row), `primary` / `alt` (normalized fire mode rows). `botPref {range [min,max] m, value 0..1}` and `noise` (m) are for bots.
- `CAT.fireModes` (`<weapon>.primary`, `<weapon>.alt`, `quick.melee`): spec FireMode schema. `type`: `hitscan` (pellets allowed), `projectile`, `beam` (continuous hitscan: `damage` per tick, `rate` ticks/s), `melee` (`reach`, `backstab`, `launch`), `cone` (`cone {range, angle}`; 360 = shockwave), `lock` (`lock {time, targets, coneDeg, range}`), `zoom` (ADS, `zoom` factor), `toggle` (`toggle {damageMult, rateMult, recoilMult, speedMult, frontResist, status}`), `block` (`block {pool, regen, parryWindow, arcDeg}`), `detonate` (detonates the owner's `projectile` of this weapon), `tool` (`tool` key in `Weapons.tools`). `trigger`: `auto`, `semi`, `burst` (`burst` rounds, `burstGap` s, `rate` = bursts/s), `charge` (fires on release; `charge {time, min, max | levels, target 'damage'|'pellets'|'multiplier', speed [min,max], ammo [..]}`), `release` (lock-on fires on release). `ammo`: `mag` / `alt` / `none`. Other fields: `falloff [startM, endM, minFactor]`, `splash {r, dmg, minFactor}`, `penetration` (`low`/`med`/`high`: thin wood/metal/glass passes at 0.5/0.7/0.85), `pierce`, `status {id, seconds, stacks}` with `statusRange` (min distance), `element`, `spinUp`, `heat` / `heatDamage`, `chain {targets, radius}`, `combo`, `stickCombo`, `split`, `puddle`, `aura`, `laser`, `cooldown`, `maxActive` + `activeRule` (`block`/`recycle`), `detonateOnRelease` (W32: a tap throws a mine on release; a hold of `DETONATE_HOLD_SECONDS` 0.35 or more detonates all the owner's mines and throws none, with or without ammo; `stepDetonateTap`), `guided`, `alwaysHead`, `knockback`, `fodderKill`, `allyLink`, `repair` (HP/s a shot gives a friendly device instead of damage: W30 beam 60; S14 also repairs the PvE core with it), `special` (`airblast`: W35 napalm also throws enemy projectiles within 5 m and 45 deg back along the aim), `noise` (-1 = weapon's), `arm` (WPN-011: metres before its projectile can explode, the larger of this and the projectile row's `arm`; `EXPLOSIVE_ALT_ARM` 6 on the P14, W21, W26, W27 and W38 explosive alts; a contact sooner is a dud, bounces still happen). `zoom` above 1 on a non-zoom alt also aims down sights while ALT is held (W19 slug 1.2x). `zoomMax` = a second scope step: while fully scoped, next/previous weapon flips between `zoom` and `zoomMax` instead of switching (`Weapons.zoomed[id]`, reset when the sights drop; W24 4x/8x). `breath` (W24 4) = a swaying scope: while aimed the view and the shots drift in a figure eight of `SCOPE_SWAY_DEG` 0.35 deg x `ads` (`Weapons.swayPitch` / `swayYaw`, added in `aimDirection` and to the view model's `recoilPitch` / `recoilYaw`); holding the Walk action steadies it to `SWAY_STEADY_SCALE` 0.1 for `breath` seconds (`Weapons.breathUsed`), then the winded sway grows to `SWAY_WINDED_SCALE` 2.5 until the actor breathes again (1 s of breath back per second). The touch `BREATH` button (action `walk`, `need: 'breath'`) shows while the player's scope sways (11a `syncBreathButton` sets the `breath` touch flag on a change only).
- `CAT.projectiles` (P01..P16 from the spec, P17 drill, P18 blade, P20 Daybreak orb (P19 is retired) - its own row so it is neither a combo target nor parryable): `speed gravity life radius bounce (speed kept) bounces (-1 until fuse, 0 = act on contact) fuse stick stickActor homing (deg/s) drag arm onHit ('explode'|'damage'|'pin' (W26: a wall within 1.5 m behind the victim pins it: status `pinned`)|'puddle'|'pull'|'drill') poolSize roll ownerSafe comboTarget parryable pierce returning color scale`.
- `CAT.recoil`: pattern parameters; `pattern` is the generated 30-entry `[pitch, yaw]` kick table in degrees (WPN-004).
- `CAT.weaponSets`: `id weapons start carry (0 = full arsenal; melee and tools never count) ammoTiers rarity power`. The rulesets use `fullSet` (all 38, shown as "Full roster"); the themed sets (Arena, Tactical, Military, Sandbox, Ravager, Knight, Loot, Exotic) are presets of mutator X10 and the custom editor, `lootSet` is every gun with rarity. Ruleset fields `carry` and `ammoTiers` (null = the set's own) keep the buy and class rulesets at a carry limit of 2 and Frame Runner on ammo tiers. `Weapons.startKeys(id)` gives the spawn guns (13d fills it from the loadout); fire mode field `tracer: false` (W06 Hush) draws no tracer line.
- `CAT.health` (HPP01..HPP09; HPP07 spawns 100 HP / 50 armor with caps 100/50 that PU20 Vital Shards raise to 200/150, and `triangle: true` turns X47 on), `CAT.precision` (HP-002 zone multipliers; MOV-025 movement spread is the movement preset's, read through `Move.inaccuracy`), `CAT.statusEffects` (`burn chill freeze shock bleed toxin blind sleep slow reveal pinned stun`; `shock` is electric and combos, `stun` is the non-electric stagger/knockdown/EMP stun), `CAT.elements` (`kinetic thermal cryo arc toxin void`), `CAT.damageKinds` (`hit melee splash beam dot self fall hazard drown telefrag crush`: what each bypasses).
- `WEAPON_RARITY_DAMAGE`, `WEAPON_RARITY_MAG` (WPN-008, rarity index 0..4).
- `CAT.attachments` (step 5.4): `id name slot ('barrel'|'magazine'|'underbarrel'|'stock', see ATTACHMENT_SLOTS) damage spread recoil reload mag reserve equip move` - multipliers, 1 = unchanged; `reload` and `equip` scale durations; stacked equip modifiers never make a swap shorter than `EQUIP_SCALE_FLOOR` 0.8 x `equipTime` (WPN-010). `WEAPON_STATS` lists the stat names in `STAT` index order.

## Weapons (11a)

- `Weapons.give(id, key, rarity, outsideCarry?)` -> bool. Owned already: adds `pickupAmmo` (WPN-007); a `dualWield` weapon picked up a second time becomes a pair instead (`Weapons.dual[slot]` = 1: magazine x2, fire rate x2, the new gun arrives loaded). At the carry limit the held (or first carried) weapon is replaced and its key left in `Weapons.dropped` (its rarity in `Weapons.droppedRarity`) for the items section to drop; `outsideCarry` skips that (a lent weapon). `Weapons.takeAway(id, weaponIndex)` removes a weapon with no drop. `Weapons.mayTake(id, weapon)` is false when a gun that would push a carried one out is offered to a person who is not holding Use, on the step Use is pressed, or while that Use hold serves another action (`Weapons.useTaken(id)`: a hook in `Weapons.useTakers` - revive within `REVIVE_HINT_RANGE` 4 m (11c `Damage.reviveInRange`), plant, defuse or relay arm (13b `Modes.usesObjective`), the royale beacon (13g) - or a press answered elsewhere, `Weapons.claimUse(id)` (14j chamber buttons and cube), or riding or leaving the skimmer; the hold stays taken until Use is released); a bot takes only an upgrade (`Weapons.botTakes`, 14e); `Weapons.swapVictim(id)` is the weapon a carry-limit pickup pushes out (the held one, else the first carried).
- `Weapons.setLoadout(id, keys)`: replaces the inventory; the last key is drawn at once. `Weapons.giveStartLoadout(id)` uses the set's `start` (called on `actor.spawn`).
- `Weapons.clear(id)`, `Weapons.has(id, key)`, `Weapons.currentWeapon(id)` -> row | null, `Weapons.ammo(id)` -> shared `{ magazine, reserve, alt, infinite }`.
- Ammo and empty triggers: `Weapons.poolLimit(id, weapon, mode, pool)` is the most one shot may ask of its pool. With nothing to refill the magazine, a shot that costs more than is left fires what is loaded (`Weapons.paidShare` < 1, `paidPellets(pellets)` for the pellet modes: W20 fires one barrel); charge levels never get cheaper. The shot that empties the magazine starts the reload by itself (`reloadIfEmpty`, a held trigger included). `Weapons.reportEmpty(id, weapon, mode)` takes the mode, not the button: it emits `weapon.empty`, and reloads unless the mode uses the alt pool (an empty alt pool only clicks). A charge or lock release short of ammo clicks empty and reloads. `payableCharge(id, weapon, mode)` is the charge fraction the pool can pay, `releaseShot(...)` the one release path for charge, lock and W32 taps; `LockOn.dropStale(id)` frees a lock on a dead or no longer hostile target.
- Dry weapons (D-F03): `reportEmpty` marks the event `dry` when nothing is left to reload from (an alt pool is always dry; a magazine is dry with no reserve and no `infiniteReserve`). For the local player (`Game.playerId`) a dry press also toasts "Out of ammo" (alt: "Alt: no ammo"), and a dry **primary** press moves on to the next owned weapon, in catalogue order, whose primary `canShoot` (a round loaded, reserve, or infinite reserve; melee and tools qualify): `switchFromDry(id, weapon)` -> `requestSwitch`, toast "Out of ammo: <name>". The Alt keeps its own pool and is never switched away from; bots and a downed actor (the Last Stand pistol) do not auto-switch (a bot has its own weapon choice, 14e). A dry tap buffered during the sprint-to-fire wait clicks once when the wait ends (`takeBufferedPress`). This is why W06, W10, W20, W26, W27 and W36 no longer sit with a dead primary while the Alt fires.
- Reload and detonate (D-F01): a reload returns before `stepAltMode`, except for a `detonate` alt: its press still runs during the reload (`stepActor`), because detonating costs no round. W40 reloads its single round at once, so Alt used to be dead with the normal loadout.
- Sprint-to-fire press buffer (D-F02, MOV-021): a trigger pressed while `Move.fireWait(id)` > 0 (the sprint-to-fire wait; the sprint was just cancelled by the press) is kept in `Weapons.pressBuffer[id]` (bit per mode) for the wait plus `PRESS_BUFFER_GRACE` (0.15 s, `pressBufferLeft`) and answered by one pull as soon as `ready()` allows (`bufferPress`, `takeBufferedPress` in `stepMode`, so primary and alt shots, every input type, a one-step tap included). A trigger still held when the wait ends fires by the normal path and drops the buffer. While a press waits, `Move.firePending[id]` = 1 keeps the sprint off (a held sprint key would otherwise resume the sprint the moment the tap ended); a weapon switch or `resetFiring` drops the buffer. Zoom, toggle and block alts are not shots and still act at once.
- Lock-on cue (D-F04): `LockOn.update(id, weapon, mode, held, dt)` counts the hold (`Weapons.lockHold`); a lock-on trigger held for the whole `lock.time` with no enemy being locked (`LockOn.tracking(id)` false) emits `weapon.nolock` once per hold (`Weapons.lockCued`), and a release-trigger lock (W31 Alt) let go with nothing locked emits it on the release (`stepRelease`); the local player also gets the toast "No target" (a bot's lock-on trigger on nothing stays silent). The hold time restarts, and the cue re-arms, whenever a lock is being tracked, so a held W07 does not say "No target" right after a kill. 17b draws the lock itself: `hudDrawLockOn` (world layer, element `crosshair`) rings every enemy of the `lockIds` slots, an arc that fills with `lockTime / lock.time` and a full red ring with a diamond once locked.
- `Weapons.addAmmo(id, ammoType, amount)` -> rounds added to every owned weapon of that type (capped at `maxAmmo`).
- `Weapons.requestSwitch(id, weaponIndex)`, `Weapons.cycle(id, +1|-1)`, `Weapons.inSlot(id, slot)`.
- State per actor (typed arrays, index = actor id): `current`, `pending`, `state` (`WEAPON_STATE.IDLE|SWAP|RELOAD`), `stateTime`, `stateDuration`, `ads` (0..1), `toggled`, `heat`, `charge`, `recoilPitch` / `recoilYaw` (deg), `bloom`, `kick`. Per actor x weapon (`Weapons.slotIndex(id, weapon.index)`): `owned`, `magazine`, `reserve`, `altAmmo`, `rarity`, `dual`. ADS rises only while a zoom alt is held and the weapon is idle (a reload lowers the sights).
- `Weapons.speedScale[id]`: movement multiplier (weapon moveMult/adsMoveMult, stance, statuses, crippled legs, downed crawl, guided warhead = 0), copied every step to `Move.weaponSpeedScale`, which S10 applies to the wish speed.
- Attachments and perks: `Weapons.attach(id, weaponKey, attachmentKey)` -> bool (weapons with a magazine that count toward the carry limit; replaces the attachment in the same slot), `Weapons.detachAll(id, weapon)`, `Weapons.modifier(id, weapon, STAT.X)` -> multiplier. Every stat = base x `Weapons.statScale[id * WEAPON_STAT_COUNT + STAT.X]` (per-actor, for perks such as reload or ammo bonuses; reset only on `actor.create`) x each fitted attachment. A newly given weapon starts bare; picking up one already owned keeps its attachments. Damage perks belong in `Damage.addAttackerScale`.
- `Weapons.spreadDegrees(id, weapon, mode)`: current spread (WPN-003, `Move.inaccuracy` x mode `moveSpread` for MOV-025, ADS, limb damage; the WPN-003 default term without a MOV-025 table applies to hitscan modes only, so projectile launchers stay exact, and W23 Lancet has `moveSpread: 0`); `ready` also asks `Move.canFire`, and every step writes `Move.weaponSpeedScale` and `Move.weaponReadyTime` (the held weapon's `readyTime`, added to `sprintToFire`, MOV-021); a damage reducer applies `Move.damageScale` (MV08 roll).
- Hooks: `Input.aimAssistTarget(id, out)` -> bool, `out = { x, y, z, target, angle }` (nearest visible, uncloaked enemy body within 6 deg / 60 m). `Render.viewModel(out)` -> `{ visible, shape (weapon class), color, kick, reload 0..1, swap 0..1 (1 = fully lowered, 0 = raised; a swap lowers the weapon in hand for the first 35 % of the swap time, 0 -> 1, then raises the pending one for the rest, 1 -> 0), ads, zoom (FOV divisor), charge (seconds), chargeFraction (0..1), recoilPitch / recoilYaw (deg the spray punch plus the scope sway move the view), dual }` for `Game.playerId`, plus the animation fields (docs/api/render.md, Weapon animation). `Render.actorAction(id, out)` gives the same for any actor's body (third person). `Render.heldWeapon(id)` follows the swap split.
- Animation state (P6, VISUAL ONLY: written by the steps, read by `Render.viewModel` / `Render.actorAction` and by nothing in the sim; the gameplay tests are unchanged): `Weapons.reloadEmpty[id]` (the reload began with an empty magazine: the empty profile), `reloadChained[id]` (this shell reload began in the step the previous one ended), `meleeAt[id]` (`Sim.time` of the hit tick: a strike is decided in the step of the trigger), `meleeCycle[id]` (the cooldown the strike added to `nextShot`: the weapon may act again `meleeCycle` after `meleeAt`), `meleeKind[id]` (`MELEE_KIND`: 1 primary, 2 alt, 3 quick), `meleeWeapon[id]` (the striker: the quick melee uses the first owned melee weapon), `meleeHit[id]` (it hit a body), `meleeCount[id]` (strikes so far), `swapFrom[id]` / `swapLevel0[id]` (the weapon drawn and how far lowered when the swap began). `Weapons.swapLook(id)` -> `{ shown, level }` (the weapon drawn and how far it is lowered: the first `SWAP_LOWER_SHARE` 0.35 of the swap time lowers the weapon drawn when the swap began, from the level it was at; the rest raises the pending one; the equip time and the switch step are unchanged), `meleeActive(id)` (a strike is in progress: it ends on the step the weapon may act again), `lastShellRound(id, weapon)`, `animationView(id, weapon, out)`, `actorAction(id, out)`.
- Melee on a surface (P6-03): a swing that finds no body but meets a wall or the floor within reach emits `impact` at the hit point (the audio row plays the material's sound; Fx adds a puff of dust; no sparks or decal) before the miss rules (W01 alt launch) run. `Weapons.meleeHit[id]` is 1 when the swing hit a body. The maul's charged slam on a floor (`impact` of `maul` from the player with `ny` above 0.6 while `meleeKind` is alt) adds 0.15 camera trauma (18k `ViewFx`).
- Registries for new behaviours: `Weapons.deliveries[type]`, `Weapons.tools[key]`, `Weapons.specials[key]`.
- `Weapons.altClaim = fn(id) -> bool` (S12 fills it: a held grenade, GAD-002): while true the ALT hold is the underhand throw and the weapon's alt ignores it until ALT is released (`Weapons.altClaimed`).
- `Weapons.rayBlockers`: list of `fn(ox, oy, oz, dx, dy, dz, maxDistance) -> distance | -1` (S12 pushes devices and ability domes); `Weapons.blockDistance(...)` returns the nearest or -1. `Shot.trace` stops a hitscan ray there (no actor behind it is hit, no penetration) and emits an `impact` at the stop facing the shooter (`emitBlockedImpact`).
- `Weapons.rateScale[id]`: the single fire-rate factor (default 1); only S12 `Buffs.applyScales` writes it (RATE status x powerup rate).
- `Input.aimAssistOverride` (filled here): the match's `rules.botSkill.aimAssist` (0..100) replaces the player's device setting; -1 or no difficulty keeps it.
- W03 parry (11d `stepBlock`): an ALT tap opens the parry window for `block.parryWindow` x `rules.botSkill.parry` / `DIFFICULTY_PARRY_BASE` (0.3 s, the default preset; no difficulty = x1), so the DIF-002 parry slider sets the player's window as it sets the PvE enemies' parry checks.
- Props: a hitscan ray (`Shot.trace`) or a projectile (`touchWorld`) that hits a prop brush (`hit.brush.prop >= 0`) damages it through `damagePropAt(hit, amount, owner)` -> `Props.damage(index, amount, owner)`; the break's `world.break` names the shooter as `source`.
- `Hitbox` lives in S08 (`08b-world-collide.js`, used by `World.raycastActors`); its sizes are the S01 body constants. `Hitbox.ray(id, ox, oy, oz, dx, dy, dz, maxDistance, inflate)` -> distance | -1 (zone in `Hitbox.zone`, `ZONE.HEAD|BODY|LEGS`; a test and console wrapper, no game code calls it); Hot form (PERF-006, no float arguments per actor): `setHitRay(ox, oy, oz, dx, dy, dz, maxDistance, inflate)` once per ray, then `Hitbox.rayOf(id)` -> true on a hit (distance in `Hitbox.distance`, zone in `Hitbox.zone`); `RayHits.collect` uses it. `Hitbox.point(id, zone, out)`. Volumes: head sphere r 0.14 at eye + 0.04, torso r 0.30 from 0.85 to 1.45 m, legs r 0.22 from 0.05 to 0.85 m, both as upright cylinders scaled by height/1.8 (zones never overlap).

## Muzzle (11e, WPN-013)

Where shots leave. `model.muzzle` ([x, y, z] in model space, `WeaponModels.weapon[weaponIndex]`) is the weapon model row's `muzzle`, else measured at load (07c `measureMuzzle`) as the front end of the foremost silhouette / far parts. `Muzzle.locate(id, weapon)` puts the world position of the muzzle in `Muzzle.point` (false: no model): for the local player (`Game.playerId`) the view model's (the `hold` to `ads` pose of its viewModels row by `Weapons.ads[id]`, the model point times `VIEWMODEL_SCALE`, in front of the eye along `Actors.yaw / pitch`), for every other actor, bots included, the held model's (the 'gun' body part pitched with the aim and sunk by the crouch, `HELD_MODEL_SCALE` about the grip). Both are pure sim state, so headless matches agree; the self-tests draw a probe model through `addWeaponModel` and `drawHeldWeapon` and compare.
- `Muzzle.reachable(id)`: no brush between the eye and `Muzzle.point`. `Muzzle.prepare(id, weapon)` works the simulated muzzle (`Muzzle.start`) and `Muzzle.usable` (a model, and the eye reaches it) out once per trigger pull (`Muzzle.newShot()`, called by `Weapons.fireShot`, and a pose / world change): every pellet, the tracer and the flash share it. **Blocked-muzzle rule (decision P2-03):** when it is not reachable the shot leaves the eye (projectiles, tracers, flashes); this also closes the old hole where a projectile started 0.45 m ahead of the eye and so beyond a wall closer than that.
- `Muzzle.launch(id, weapon, direction, converge)` -> `Muzzle.launchFrom { x, y, z, dx, dy, dz }`: the start (muzzle, else eye) and the unit flight direction. With `converge` the direction runs from the muzzle to the point under the crosshair: `Muzzle.aimDistance` = the nearest wall (`World.raycast`) or actor (`RayHits.collect`, allies too) on the eye ray `direction`, up to `MUZZLE_CONVERGE_RANGE` 120 m; a wall or actor nearer than `MUZZLE_MIN_CONVERGE` 1.5 m on that ray means the shot leaves the eye along `direction` (the muzzle reaches 0.5 to 0.9 m ahead and must not be behind what is aimed at). `Projectiles.spawnFromWeapon` and `spawnBallistic` call it (`converge` = the projectile row has no gravity, or it is an X41 bullet, which drops little at 320 m/s and so must hit what a hitscan shot hits; a lobbed row, gravity of `LOB_MIN_GRAVITY` 9 m/s2 or more, does not converge but `Muzzle.solveLob(id, direction, speed, gravity)` (P8-04, F0-13) writes the low ballistic arc from the muzzle through the point under the crosshair into `launchFrom`, returning false (the eye direction stays) when no point is under the crosshair, it is under 1.5 m away or out of reach of the speed; lightly dropping rows, shards and stakes, keep the eye direction and only start at the muzzle; `BotCombat.dropFor` adds no drop for a lobbed row). The way from the muzzle to the aim point is checked too (the whole line for a converging shot, 2 m for a lobbed one, `MUZZLE_PATH_SLACK` 0.3 m short of the aim point): a pillar edge the eye ray clears sends the shot out of the eye.
- Hitscan and beam damage are traced from the eye along the aim ray as before (hit results, falloff and penetration do not change); `drawTracer` and the muzzle flash (`announceShot`, the cone burst) start at `Muzzle.visual(id, weapon)`: the muzzle, or the eye when it is blocked. For the local player the visual point scales the muzzle's right / up offsets by `tan(fov / 2)` of the world view over the view model's (`Muzzle.viewFovRatio`), because the view model is drawn with `video.viewmodelFov`; the simulation never reads the fields of view. Drawn only (`Muzzle.placeDrawn`): the player's view model offset (`Render.config.viewmodelOffset`, fading with the ADS share), the left gun of a W04 pair (alternating with `Weapons.recoilShot`), and a held weapon follows the body scale (`readActorStyle(id).scale`). A tracer whose shot ended closer than `MUZZLE_MIN_CONVERGE` starts at the eye.

## Projectiles (11b)

- `Projectiles.spawn(row, owner, weaponIndex, modeIndex, x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, damage)` -> slot; `Projectiles.spawnFromWeapon(...)` starts at the muzzle (see Muzzle).
- Gravity and drag are per projectile (`Projectiles.gravity[i]`, `Projectiles.drag[i]`), copied from the row at spawn.
- X41 Ballistics (`rules.fx.ballistics { gravity, drag }`): `deliverHitscan` spawns every pellet with `Projectiles.spawnBallistic(owner, weapon, mode, modeIndex, direction, damage, ballistics)`: row `bullet` (P21, 320 m/s), life = mode range / speed, gravity and drag from the mutator; the hit keeps the hitscan mode (falloff by distance flown, headshots). Pierce, thin-cover penetration, chain and combo shots do not apply to bullets. `Weapons.ballistics()` = the `{ gravity, drag }` in force or null; `Weapons.fliesAsBullet(mode)` = this hitscan mode fires bullets now (weapon.fire listeners that settle shots with an instant ray skip those). A bullet stops at `Weapons.blockDistance` on each sweep step (devices, domes; an impact shows the stop), and `Projectiles.bulletHits` = list of `fn(owner, mode, ox, oy, oz, dx, dy, dz, length)` runs for the part of a step the bullet flew unstopped by bodies (S12: devices take one pellet, X30 ammo blows up).
- W17 combo: `Projectiles.comboTarget(ox, oy, oz, dx, dy, dz, maxDistance)` -> index of the nearest flying `comboTarget` projectile a hitscan ray meets (its distance in `Projectiles.comboDistance`), else -1; `Projectiles.detonateCombo(i, shooter, mode)` detonates it (replaces `comboRay`).
- `Projectiles.countOwned(owner, weaponIndex, typeIndex = -1)` (typeIndex = projectile row index, -1 any; `maxActive` limits count only the mode's own projectile row, so W32 mines never recycle grenades), `Projectiles.detonateOwned(owner, weaponIndex, projectileKey)`, `Projectiles.clear()`, `Projectiles.active` (count for the perf HUD), `Projectiles.guiding[id]`.
- Pool: 256 slots, struct of arrays; at a row's `poolSize` (or a full pool) the oldest is recycled. Projectiles never strike their owner directly (own splash still applies).
- Render sources: `projectiles` (spheres, puddles as flat cylinders) and `gates` (rings).

## Damage (11c)

- `Damage.apply(source, target, info)` -> damage dealt. The only damage path. `info = Damage.info()` (pooled; fields `amount zone ('head'|'body'|'legs'|'none') stomach mode weapon kind element distance knockback bypassArmor bypassShield x y z dx dy dz`). Order: friendly fire / self scale -> `Damage.addAttackerScale` hooks -> zone x precision -> falloff -> shatter -> `Damage.addReducer` hooks (subtractive reducers see the zone-scaled amount; HPP03 applies the zone after the shields instead) -> overshield -> shield (element factor) -> armor -> health -> minimum 1 -> downed / death.
- `Damage.statusHooks`: `fn(id, row, source, seconds)` after `Damage.addStatus` lands a status (filled by S12 12f for the HP-005 combos: wet + Shock, Shock chain, Burn melts ice; and 12h for the floor patches).
- `Damage.explode(blast)` with `blast = Damage.blast()`: `{ x, y, z, owner, mode, radius, damage, exclude, ownerSafe, weapon, element }`. Linear falloff to `mode.splash.minFactor` (0.2), line of sight, self damage x `rules.selfDamage`, knockback.
- HP-007 downed state: in a team match an actor at 0 HP goes down instead of dying while a teammate still stands, when the health preset has `downed` (HPP06 loot) or the ruleset does (`rules.downed`: RS04 modern, RS05 pilot, RS09 heroes, RS12 frame); never PvE enemies. Downed: crawl (`DOWNED_SPEED`), finished after 100 more damage or 30 s bleed-out, and at once when no teammate stands any more. A teammate holding `BTN.USE` within `REVIVE_REACH` (1.5 m) for `REVIVE_SECONDS` (5 s) revives it at 30 HP (`Damage.reviving[id]`, `Damage.reviveProgress[id]` for the HUD); `Damage.nearestDownedAlly(id, maxDistance)` is the one search (A27, the Use revive, bot revive goals); `Damage.downedCount` = downed actors after the last step. `Damage.downedWeapon = fn(id) -> weapon key | ''` (S13 fills it: the Last Stand perk's `weapon`, W04): the downed actor draws it and keeps firing (`Damage.lastStand[id]`; `Weapons.lowered(id)` is false), and the old weapon comes back on revive. `Damage.downHooks` = fn(id) list run as an actor goes down (S13: `Modes.dropCarried`, so a downed carrier drops its flag, charge or relic). A downed actor lies prone (S10 forces `STANCE.PRONE` on the ground; the crawl speed stays `DOWNED_SPEED`, with no stance factor; no jumps and no special moves). A downed bot holds still for its reviver (it still aims and fires); a reviving bot holds Use while the ally is within `REVIVE_REACH`. A pistol not owned is lent outside the carry limit (`Damage.lastStandLent[id]`, nothing is dropped) and taken back on revive or death.
- `Damage.resetHealth(id)` (on `actor.spawn` / `actor.create`), `Damage.revive(id)`, `Damage.addStatus(id, key, source, seconds, stacks)`, `Damage.hasStatus(id, key)`, `Damage.statusSpeed(id)`, `Damage.limbSpread(id)`.
- Healing: `Damage.regenerate(id, amount, cap)` -> true when it healed is the shared heal step (regen, health pickups, PU18, abilities, cards, the X47 triangle kill heal); a few capped heals still write health directly (the regen mutator, PU vital and bands, PvE champion and support heals). `Damage.overhealCap(id)` = max(own max health, the preset's `overhealCap`), the one overheal cap (PU18 souls, C20 Soul Feast, health-scale changes). `Damage.lifesteal(damageEvent, share)` heals the event's living source by `share` x the amount dealt to another actor, up to its max health (PU12 Vampire Fang, X13 Vampire, C24 Life Thief all call it).
- Shield regen pause: `Damage.pauseShieldRegen(id, seconds)` keeps the later of the current block and now + seconds; `Damage.shieldRegenBlocked(id)` -> bool (S12 suppression and EMP; ability shields check it too).
- Per-actor arrays other sections may write: `Damage.surplus` (A40 Rally Cry), `Damage.shieldElement` (PvE coloured shield: elements index + 1), `Damage.helmet` (HPP04 buy), `Damage.shieldRegenBlockedUntil` (Sim.tick before which shields do not recharge; S12 EMP, health regeneration is not affected; reset on spawn). `Damage.poolOverride = fn(id, out {health, armor, shield})` for hero pools. `Damage.roleOf = fn(id) -> role` for the HPP05 role regen (S12 fills it with the hero role; without it the profile role is used). `Damage.applyCount` (self-test).
- A freeze cannot be shattered by hits in the step that froze the target (the other pellets of one freezing blast); the next step's hit shatters it. Own damage over time (`dot`) is scaled by `rules.selfDamage` like own splash. Puddle statuses ride on accepted puddle damage only (friendly fire and protection apply). W03 block and parry count only while the block weapon is drawn and idle (block also needs ALT held); swaps, deaths and respawns drop the guard. `WeaponSpecialState.parryHooks` (fn(target, projectile) -> true) lets other sections parry a parryable projectile first (S14: PvE green projectiles inside the difficulty parry window, any weapon); a true answer reflects it like a W03 parry. Stuns (freeze, shock, sleep, stun) set `ACTOR_FLAG.FROZEN`; downed sets `ACTOR_FLAG.DOWNED`. Fodder = `kind ENEMY` with `profile.fodder === true`; `profile.massFactor` scales knockback; `profile.role === 'support'` enables the HPP05 passive regen.

## Events

P7: class rows carry `flash`, `casing`, `tail` and `thirdKick`, weapon rows `flash` (-1 = the class's), `casing` (`'none'` opts out) and `reloadFx` (`vent`, `flare`); `linkWeapon` fills `muzzleFlash` and `casingKind`. New events: `weapon.cue` (the local player's reload and cycle marks), `weapon.parry`, `weapon.chargeFull` (see ARCHITECTURE). The pictures and sounds are 11f, 06b and 17; none of it reaches the sim.

Emitted: `weapon.fire` `{ id, weapon, weaponClass, mode ('primary'|'alt'|'melee' for the MELEE button bash), x, y, z, dx, dy, dz }`, `weapon.reload` / `weapon.switch` `{ id, weapon }`, `weapon.empty` `{ id, weapon, pool: 'mag'|'alt', dry }`, `weapon.nolock` `{ id, weapon }`, `impact` `{ x, y, z, nx, ny, nz, material, weapon, owner }`, `explosion` `{ x, y, z, radius, owner, source, element }`, `damage` `{ target, source, amount, absorbed, zone, weapon, element, kind, shieldDamage, armorDamage, healthDamage, x, y, z, dx, dy, dz }`, `kill` `{ victim, killer, assister, weapon, zone, element, kind (the damage kind of the killing blow: 'melee' for the quick-melee bash too), x, y, z }` (a bleed-out kill carries the weapon key of whoever downed the victim, `Damage.downedWith`, and `'bleedout'` only when none is known; assister = most recent other damager within 5 s; environment kills credit the last attacker), `impulse` `{ id, x, y, z }`, `sound` `{ x, y, z, radius, kind: 'shot'|'explosion', source }`, `projectile.incoming` `{ x, y, z, kind, source }` (once per projectile of a row with `incoming`: rocket, warhead, missile, from a non-ally within 25 m of the local player and closing in).

Consumed: `damage.request` (routed through `Damage.apply`), `actor.create`, `actor.spawn`, `kill` (drops the victim's block), `match.start` / `match.stop` (clear projectiles).

## Known limits

Twin gates teleport (floor gates place the feet on the floor, ceiling gates the head under the ceiling, wall gates the middle 0.8 m out) but are drawn as rings without a view through them. The Graviton Lifter pushes, pulls and catches projectiles, hand grenades (`WeaponSpecialState.grenadeHook`, S12 `Thrown`: the nearest flying or resting grenade in the 30 degree cone, thrown back as the holder's own with its fuse running; `heldGrenade[id]`) and bodies or breakable props (`propHook` = S08 `PropLift`, at most 150 kg, launched at 30 m/s for 0.5 x mass x speed damage capped at 150). Hitscan shots damage the prop they hit (`Props.damage`). Attachments have data, stats and tests but no pick-up or menu UI yet. Not built yet: the W24 second zoom level (8x) and its 4 s hold breath (the scope has one 4x level and no sway to steady). The HP-005 floor combos (wet floor + Shock, oil floor + Burn) are S12 floor patches (12h `FloorSurfaces`, docs/api/abilities-economy.md). Also not built yet (audit item 32 stays open): no hand-made or generated map places `map.props`, so in real play the lifter's prop lift and MAP-013 prop damage only meet props in tests; crates go into arena maps 16b-16e once nav (09a) and map validation (16v) handle dynamic brushes.
