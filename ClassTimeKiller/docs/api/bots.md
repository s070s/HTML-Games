# Bots (S14) - bot brains, team play and difficulty

Files: `src/03g-data-bots.js` (tiers, traits, roster, barks, role splits), `src/03h-data-difficulty.js` (difficulty presets and slider ranges), `src/14a-bots.js` (lifecycle, think loop, motor, difficulty stage), `src/14b-bots-perception.js` (sight, hearing, damage, memory), `src/14c-bots-move.js` (paths, steering, links, stuck recovery), `src/14d-bots-combat.js` (targets, aim, fire, weapons, footwork, grenades, abilities), `src/14e-bots-decide.js` (utility goals, objective planners), `src/14f-bots-team.js` (roles, pings, orders, barks), `src/14g-bots-debug.js` (F9 view). Tests: `src/20b-test-bots.js` (group `bots`, about 8 s; the slow group `botsoak`, about 60 s, runs only with `node tools/selftest.mjs --slow` or `--group botsoak`: see Tests below). Spec: BOT-001..BOT-100, DIF-001..DIF-003, ARC-033, PERF-009, PERF-011.

## Contract

- A bot writes only the input fields of `Actors` (`moveX moveZ aimYaw aimPitch buttons slotRequest`), in the Sim system `botThink` (order 10), like the player's input layer. Every other system treats it like a person (BOT-001).
- A brain runs for an actor of kind `BOT` from its first `actor.spawn` on. Actors made with `Actors.create` and never spawned (tests, dummies) are not driven.
- Same seed, same match: at `match.setup` every typed array of `Bots BotSense BotMove BotCombat BotDecide BotTeam` goes back to its load-time contents (14f), so nothing an earlier match left behind steers the next one (a `bots` test plays a seed, another match, then the seed again).
- `Actors.profile` stays what S13 stored (the `CAT.bots` row or `null`); `Bots.profile[id]` holds the row the brain uses.
- No cheating (ARC-033): targets come only from perception (seen, heard, hurt by, pinged by a team-mate) and its memory. A target out of sight is judged by where the bot last perceived it (`BotSense.perceivedPosition`), for distance, aggression, retreat and cover. `BotCombat.fairness` counts every bot shot: `shots`, `unperceived` (the shot does not come from a trigger decision made with the target in sight and the aim on it; a burst's later rounds and the shot that kills count with their decision) and `unknown` (the target was not even remembered). The soak and the mode matches check both stay 0.

## Think loop (per tick, per living bot)

1. `BotSense.serve(now)`: perception passes that are due (20 Hz in combat near the player, 10 Hz otherwise, 5 Hz beyond 40 m or outside the player's view; staggered by id). Budget: 96 rays per tick over all bots for sight and hearing together (minus `BotSense.reservedRays`, the PvE enemies' share while they run), combat pairs first; sight leaves the last 16 for sounds (a sound beyond the through-wall radius needs a ray; none left = not heard).
2. `BotTeam.step(now)`: the commander (roles every 15 s). The player's ping button and comm wheel are read by `CommWheel` (17g).
3. Per bot: decision at 2 Hz (staggered), combat (target, aim, fire, weapon), navigation (path following), combat footwork (strafe, dodge), goal actions (hold, use), team updates (acknowledgements, barks), then the motor (look spring with the tier's turn cap, move axes, buttons).
- The first 0.5 s after a spawn the bot only looks along its facing (BOT-002 Spawn state). Frozen actors (`ACTOR_FLAG.FROZEN`, freeze time) wait the same way.

## Match setup

- `setup.bots[n]`: `{ profile (CAT.bots key or row), team, tier (0..7, 'BT3' or 'skilled'), name, dummy }`. Tier: `entry.tier`, else the roster row's tier, else an even mix over the difficulty's `tierMin..tierMax`; then the adaptive shift.
- `setup.difficulty`: a preset id, or `{ preset, <slider field>: value, tierMin, tierMax }`. The `difficulty` rule stage writes `rules.botSkill` (the merged row, `preset` = its id). An unknown preset logs one error and plays `regular`.
- Settings: `difficulty.botReaction` (0.5..2) and `difficulty.botAimError` (0.5..3) scale every bot; `difficulty.adaptive` turns on DIF-003 (after a match against bots: +1 tier when the player won, -1 when the player lost; team modes judge the player's team by `results.winnerTeam`, a draw changes nothing; an endless or range run is neither a win nor a loss, `OPEN_ENDED_MODES` in 14a; free-for-all: first or last; kept in `difficulty.tierShift`, shown as `match.results.botTierChange`); `gameplay.chatter` (`off minimal normal`), `gameplay.taunts`. The F9 view reads `developer.botDebug` (defined by S18).

## API

### Bots (14a)

- `Bots.isBot(id)`, `Bots.tier(id)` (the `CAT.botTiers` row), `Bots.trait(id, TRAIT.<key>)`, `Bots.state[id]` (`BOT_STATE`, names in `BOT_STATE_NAMES`), `Bots.profile[id]`.
- `Bots.lookAt(id, x, y, z)`, `Bots.wish(id, dx, dz, amount)`, `Bots.press(id, BTN.*)`: what the layers below use; the motor turns them into input. Per-step steering (14c `steer(id, now)` / `avoidance(id)`, 14d strafe and dodge) writes the move into the scratch `botWish` (`x z amount`) and calls `Bots.wishFrom(id)` (PERF-006: three float arguments to a call that is not inlined are boxed every step). Sight and hearing rays go through `senseLine` and `World.lineClearOf`; the smoke test runs only while a smoke volume exists.
- The motor never makes a double-tap dodge: a move axis that would read as one stays just under the tap threshold (`Bots.keepUnderTap`).
- `Bots.reactionScale()`, `Bots.aimErrorScale()`, `Bots.maxAttackers()`, `Bots.inGrace(id, target)` (BOT-042).
- `Difficulty.resolve(choice)`, `Difficulty.adapt(match)`, `Difficulty.tierShift()`.

### Perception (14b)

- `BotSense.sees(bot, other)`, `BotSense.knows(bot, other, now)` (memory within 10 s), `BotSense.alerted(bot)`, `BotSense.viewRange(bot)` (against a standing, moving actor).
- `BotSense.perceivedPosition(bot, other, out)`: the live position while in sight, else the remembered one (BOT-040); fills and returns `out`.
- Per pair (`BotSense.pair(bot, other)`): `visible source seenAt knownAt knownX/Y/Z knownVX/VZ awareness reactAt`.
- Per bot: `heardAt heardX/Y/Z attacker attackedAt alert`.
- `BotSense.share(bot, other, x, y, z, now)`: a team-mate's information (enemy pings).
- `BotSense.stats`: `rays raysThisTick maxRaysPerTick deferred heard hearingRays` (`raysThisTick` runs from one sight pass to the next, so a tick's sounds count with it).
- Inputs: `sound` events (`radius` x (0.5 + hearing trait); farther than 60% of that needs a clear line), `damage` events (direction and rough distance of the attacker), `Smoke.lineBlocked` for sight, `kill` and `actor.remove` (forget).
- Smoke blocks sight like a wall, except for a bot whose hero has the H07 `smokeVision` passive (`Smoke.lineBlocked` gets the bot as viewer). A cloaked actor (`ACTOR_FLAG.INVISIBLE`, `Items.perceptionScale`) is seen only much closer. A decoy's shot is a `sound` with the owner as `source`: the owner is remembered where the decoy stands.
- `BotSense.mine[bot]`: the nearest enemy placed gadget within 8 m that the bot sees (`Gadgets.visibleTo`, one budgeted ray each; friendly devices skipped), -1 none. An enemy `grenade` sound within 6 m sets `dangerUntil / dangerX / dangerZ` (run from it for 1.5 s) when the throwable is harmful: the bounce sound carries the row's `effect` (S12), checked against `BOT_FLEE_EFFECTS` (smoke, flash, decoys and darts are not run from).
- Actors in a `BOT_HUNTED_ROLES` role are remembered as pinged while the bot could see them (their marker is on everyone's screen).

### Navigation (14c)

- `BotMove.goTo(id, x, y, z, radius)`: follow a path there (a destination more than 2 m from where the current path was asked for gets a new path, at most every 0.75 s; a newer destination waits); `stop(id)`, `arrived(id)`, `failed(id)`, `distanceToDestination(id)`.
- `BotMove.configure(id)`: path options from the bot's movement preset and tier (jump height with extra jumps only for tiers with `jumps`, speed, gravity).
- Links: walk, crouch (holds CROUCH), jump (jumps again whenever grounded short of the landing, extra jump near the apex for high links), drop, ladder (walks into the ladder volume, then climbs facing the top), jump pad and teleporter (walk in), swim (rises, mantles out over a rim). Water exits: S09 links a swim exit only where a knee-height bank face lets the swimmer mantle out, so every linked exit starts open. An exit found blocked in play is marked (1000 s extra on paths from the water) and another taken; a swimmer whose goal is a blocked exit swims to the nearest open one first. A bot that falls in re-plans from the water.
- Steering: on walk, crouch and swim segments the bot heads for a point 2 m ahead along the path (no grinding along door frames); a missed landing or a fall off a ramp edge re-plans at once.
- Stuck (BOT-012): progress is a path point reached, getting closer to the point ahead, or climbing a ladder. No progress for 1.5 s: alternate jump-and-strafe and backing off; 3 s: repath avoiding the node ahead; 6 s: give the goal up. `stuckSeconds`, `longestStuck`, `log` (last 16 give-ups).
- Trap memory (all bots): a node a bot had to repath around costs 30 s extra for 60 s; water nodes cost 4 s extra, so bots swim only when it saves real time.
- Ledge guard (BOT-010, `guardLedge`, `guardHop`): after every move layer a grounded bot about to walk, strafe or slide over an edge with no floor within its drop limit brakes, and a combat hop (no jump link) with no floor `LEDGE_HOP_SECONDS` ahead is dropped; jump, pad, ladder and teleporter links and weapon jumps are exempt.
- Pad ride: a jump pad throw sets `BotMove.padRideUntil`; until the bot lands (or that time) it does not steer or jump.
- `BotMove.ownsView(id)`: on a ladder, a water exit or a jump up onto a ledge the movement owns the view and combat footwork waits.

### Combat (14d)

- `BotCombat.lookAtKnown(id, other)` turns toward the chest of the other actor's remembered position (used by `lookAtThreat` and `watch`).
- `BotCombat.target[id]`, `BotCombat.errorDeg[id]`, `BotCombat.totalShots/totalHits[id]` (per match), `BotCombat.fairness`.
- Aim (BOT-030): the tier's `aimError` (x accuracy trait, target and own speed, 2x over the first 0.6 s of tracking, difficulty and settings), smooth value noise every 0.4 s, critically damped look spring with feed-forward of the moving aim point, capped by the tier's turn rate. Head aim from tier 4 with precise weapons, feet for splash with aim skill > 0.6, lead for projectiles (linear above 0.4 aim skill, refined above 0.8). Under X41 Ballistics hitscan shots fly as bullets, so bots lead them at the bullet speed and aim above the target by the bullet drop (`BotCombat.leadSpeed`, `dropFor`; gravity from `rules.fx.ballistics`).
- Target choice: a downed enemy that cannot shoot (`BotCombat.harmless`) scores `TARGET_DOWNED_SHARE` (0.3) of a target, only while a standing enemy is in sight; with none in sight bots finish it off.
- Recoil control (BOT-031): pulls against 30% (BT0) to 100% (BT7) of the aim punch, x 0.75..1.25 by the spray-control trait.
- Hit cap (BOT-093): over a 30 s window the hit rate is held near the tier's `hitCap` by scaling the error.
- Weapons: best owned weapon for the distance by `botPref` (+ favourite bonus), switch when safe or empty, reload when safe or empty. In a fight a switch needs a gun worth `COMBAT_SWITCH_GAIN` more and then rests `COMBAT_SWITCH_REST` (3 s) from its first slot press. One value rule, `BotCombat.weaponValue(id, weapon, share)`, serves the draw, the pickup choice and the buy. A map gun is valued by how much it beats the best gun the bot owns (`BotCombat.bestOwnedGun(id)`; 14e `weaponItemValue` scales the gain by `WEAPON_UPGRADE_PULL` 4, and the 14f shop uses the same measure), and bots ignore map guns when the mode hands out the guns (`Economy.modeArmsEveryone()`: Arms Ladder, Roulette). So the Warden spawn pistol scores about a third of Arena Classic kills, not about 60%. At the carry limit a bot swaps only to a gun it values above the one it would drop (`Weapons.botTakes`, 14e, against `Weapons.swapVictim`), never in Arms Ladder or Roulette; a royale loot gun it would refuse is worth 0 to it. Fire only when aligned with the wanted aim and after the reaction time. Head aim only when the expected miss at that range is under 0.3 m. Semi and burst triggers wait until the residual recoil (after recoil control) is under 1 deg.
- Charge and lock triggers: held until charged, released with the target in sight and the aim on it. Aim off or target hidden: the trigger stays held (letting go would fire at nothing); 1.5 s unseen, a weapon-switch tap drops the charge (a bot with one weapon keeps holding).
- Footwork: strafe (6.5% side change per 0.4 s check), keep the weapon's range, back off while reloading, dodge seen projectiles (tier `dodgeChance`, at most 60%). A seen enemy mine is passed crouched when crouching keeps it quiet (within its trigger + 1.2 m), else steered around: from 5 m beyond its trigger radius a path that runs into the ring of trigger + 1.2 m gets a sidestep along the ring (weight 2 against the path's 1, on the side the bot stands as seen from its destination) and, inside the ring, a push out, until the path direction clears the ring. A heard harmful grenade is run from.

### Kit use (14d: grenades, gadgets, abilities)

- `BOT_USE_TRIGGERS`: the `when` words (`always enemyVisible enemyClose underFire lowHealth allyLow allyDowned escape moving groupedEnemies objective cover coverOrHolding holding lowAmmo`). Each tests only what the bot perceives. An ability row's `botUse { when, range, chance }` and a `BOT_GADGET_USE` row both name one; a use goes when the trigger holds, the target (if any) is in `range` and the chance passes.
- Pacing: every 0.5 s while nothing is wanted; after a use, 4 s (grenade), 2 s (gadget) or 1.5 s (ability) at abilityUse 0.5, shorter with the trait. One grenade per team every 3 s (`BotTeam.takeGrenadeToken`).
- Grenades: the selected thrown row. The landing is where the bot believes its target is (`aim: 'enemy'`) or half way there (`'between'`, a screen). A carried smoke (effect `smoke`, carried even while another type is selected) also goes, whatever its trigger, at the nearest choke hint on the way of a bot whose goal is ATTACK or CHASE: within 4 m of the line to where it believes the target is, inside the smoke row's `range` and 3 m or more short of the target; once per 15 s per bot (`BotCombat.chokeSmokedAt`). When another type is selected (a class loadout gives its lethal first), the bot presses `BTN.CYCLE_GRENADE` like a player and throws at a later kit check, once the smoke is selected; it does not cycle back (bots cycle for nothing else): the smoke stays selected until the last one is thrown, then Gadgets selects the next carried type. The throw solver finds the low-arc view pitch (up to 35 deg) for the real throw (`GADGET_TUNING.throwSpeed / throwLift`, gravity, from the eye): each pitch's flight is closed-form, the pitch itself is found by bisection (16 steps), since the fixed upward lift leaves no closed form for it (BOT-034 says "analytically"). The view turns onto the arc at the tier's turn rate; then GRENADE is held for the cook time (fuse minus flight minus 0.3 s, cookable rows only) and released.
- Gadgets (BTN.GADGET): placed and deployed rows by `BOT_GADGET_USE`. Mines, turrets, beacons and barriers go down only where the bot holds its spot (`BotDecide.holding`); the jammer goes down at an objective with a fight on it.
- Abilities: `passive` and `ult` rows need no press. `hold` rows are held while the trigger lasts (at most 3 s). A `channel` is started only where the bot holds its spot, and the bot stands still while it runs (`BotCombat.channeling(id)`). Toggles and channels are pressed off once their trigger ends (at most 4 s on; see the known limit below); an off press is one tick long and one that did not take is pressed again 0.5 s later (`switchRetryAt`), so every press is a new edge. An ultimate whose `botUse.when` aims it at enemies (`enemyVisible enemyClose groupedEnemies`) also goes with 2+ enemies in sight or a contested objective, within its own `range` (BOT-035); escape and self-buff ultimates keep their own trigger.
- Weapon jumps (tier `weaponJumps`): with no target, a grounded bot with the weaponJumper trait above neutral, a splash primary and 60%+ health whose goal is 2.5 m+ up within 8 m, with no walkable way there (the path failed or ends below the goal) or only a detour 3x the straight line or longer, turns away from the goal, looks down (70 deg) and presses JUMP and FIRE together, so the blast lifts it toward the goal; at most once per 6 s. A failed path keeps its goal while the jump is wanted or aimed, and path following leaves the view to the jump.
- Movement abilities (BOT-010): a grapple or blink goes only where its end point has floor within the bot's drop limit, never over the void; a glide is held only while there is floor `GLIDE_LOOKAHEAD` (6 m) along the move.
- `BotCombat.uses`: `{ abilities, grenades, gadgets, weaponJumps }`, presses by all bots this match (reset at `match.setup`; a gadget press the gadget code refuses still counts).
- Mutators (MUT-003, `Mutators.fx`): head-only rules aim for the head; airborne-only rules fire at a grounded target only with splash; one-hit rules raise dodging (still capped at 60%); no fall damage lets paths drop from any height (14c); damage dealt / taken scale aggression (x0.7..1.3, 14e).
- Known limit: S12 `Abilities.step` reads no input while a slot is active, so the off press does nothing today; channels are therefore kept to held spots.

### Decisions (14e)

- `GOAL` (`NONE ROAM ATTACK CHASE RETREAT COLLECT OBJECTIVE CAMP INVESTIGATE ORDER REVIVE SQUAD`; REVIVE = BOT-071: walk to the nearest downed teammate within 40 m and hold Use beside it, only while no enemy the bot sees (a downed one that cannot shoot does not count) is within 10 m of it, and only one reviver per ally; a revive already under way skips that safety check; within `REVIVE_CLOSE` 20 m it outranks every objective but a carry (`REVIVE_URGENT`), state REVIVE; SQUAD = BOT-052, scored by 14f: see Team), names in `GOAL_NAMES`. `BotDecide.goal[id]`, `goalScore`, `goalX/Y/Z`, `goalAction` (`ACTION.MOVE HOLD USE`), `goalTarget`, `scores` (last score per goal, F9 view).
- Every goal scores 0..1; the best wins unless the current one is within 10% (BOT-020). A goal whose path failed rests for 5 s; with nothing wanted the bot holds and looks around (never without a goal).
- Fleeing and aggression (BOT-021) are kept apart. `BotDecide.flees(id, target)` decides fleeing: the enemy is 6 m or more higher, or (without a powerup) health is under 45% x (0.5 + self-preservation trait) with armour under 40, the bot is unarmed, or the enemy's weapon is worth 0.45 more (`botPref.value`). It retreats (to health, cover from `Nav.nearestCover`, or away) from its target while in sight, else from the enemy that hit it in the last 3 s, judged by where it believes that enemy is. `BotDecide.aggression(id)` only scales how hard it fights: at least 0.7 with a powerup; else max(0.55, weapon `botPref.value`) x (0.5 + aggression trait) x (0.5 + difficulty aggression) x the mutator factor.
- Items (BOT-013): map `items` for the mode, valued by need (health by missing health, armour, unowned weapons, powerups, ammo when low), reachable only, taken spots skipped for 20 s; tiers with `itemTiming` and item focus > 0.5 arrive 1..3 s before a timed respawn (`Items.timers`).
- Objective planners, by `Modes.rules.objective` (BOT-080): `flag` (ctf / oneflag: take, carry home, return, escort, defend by role; raiders gather and push by `BOT_FLAG_PUSH`, a carrier outranks every fight), `point` and `hill` (spread over the zone, defenders hold owned ones), `bombsite` (carry and plant, escort, defuse), the elimination safe area and the royale ring. Then the add-on modes by their goal hints (`Modes.goalFor` -> `BOT_OBJECTIVE_VERBS`: relays, the convoy, relics, the flame, the curse beacon), and royale loot (`Modes.loot`) instead of map pickups. M20 squads (`Modes.squads.count > 0`), right after the ring: with a squadmate out and a beacon use left, every standing member goes to hold Use at the squad's beacon (urgency 0.95; not while the ring has left the beacon outside); else a member more than 12 m from its squad leader (the player when standing in the squad, else the lowest standing id) goes back to it (urgency up to 0.8 at 24 m). As an objective plan it scores 0.6 or more, so it outranks chasing, roaming and loot: a bot with a target keeps fighting until it is more than 24 m from its leader. Bots sent to one spot spread over it (golden-angle offset by id). `BotDecide.registerPlanner(kind, fn(id, plan, now))` adds one.
- States (BOT-002): the goal's state, DOWNED while the bot is downed (HP-007, it holds still for its reviver).
- Attack (BOT-053): only the holders of the target's attack tokens, and only with aggression of `AGGRESSION_CLOSE_IN` (0.4) or more, close in to 80% of the weapon range; the others fight from where they stand, coming no nearer than the full range (a melee weapon always closes in).
- Chase and search: a CHASE goal shows as state SEARCH once the bot's memory of its target is older than 2 s. At the last known position with nothing there, the guess moves on to `SEARCH_SPOTS` (2) nearby spots in turn (a random walk of `SEARCH_HOPS` nav links each, `BotDecide.searchLeft[id]`; the goal and a fresh path move to each spot at once, so the bot walks there); then the memory is dropped and the bot roams. Tiers with `flanks` route a chase 12 m+ long through the `BOT_FLANK_HINT` spot giving the shortest way that is at most 1.6x the straight one (then 20 s rest), while it holds its team's flank token.
- Hold sweep: a bot holding its spot swings its view `HOLD_LOOK_SPEED` (0.6 rad/s) over `HOLD_LOOK_ARC` (60 deg) either side of a centre set on arrival: out of the held objective (the way the bot faced when within 1 m of its centre), or on the last aim after a fight or a hit; a retreat centres on the threat.
- Per-map lookups (`BotDecide.placeNodes()`: the nav node of every map item and royale loot spot, and in 14c/14f the water, exits and flag rally points) run at match start and again on `nav.ready`, when a sliced nav build finishes after it.
- Camping (BOT-041): camper trait > 0.5 and a strong weapon; spots from `BOT_CAMP_HINTS` that see no enemy spawn within 50 m; 120 + 180 x camper seconds, then 60 s cooldown.
- `BotDecide.carriesObjective(actor)`, `contested(id)`, `favoriteWeapon(id)`, `hurry(id, now)`, `goalFailed(id, now)`.

### Team (14f)

- `ROLE` (`FREELANCE ATTACK DEFEND ESCORT`), `BotTeam.role[id]`; the bots with the most teamwork take the support roles.
- Orders (BOT-051): a `ping` from a person is an order by its `COMM_CALLOUTS` row (14f; a kind with no row = go): `attack`, `defend` and `go` at the spot are taken by the 2 nearest team bots; `cover` (Cover me: hold within `COVER_RADIUS` 4 m of the caller, 45 s or until they die) by the 2 nearest too; `follow` (follow the caller, 60 s or until they die), `hold` (defend where each bot stands) and `regroup` (go to the caller) by every team bot. A ping with `to` = a team bot (the comm wheel was opened aiming at it, `BotTeam.addressee`) is taken by that bot only. Replies (`at: 'reply'`: `dibs`, `no`, `onMyWay`) order nobody. Every taker acknowledges 0.5..1.5 s later (`orderAck` bark) and follows it as goal `ORDER` (`ORDER_SECONDS`: defend holds 45 s; attack / go 30 s or until there). `BotTeam.orderGivenAt / answeredAt / orderSource[id]`.
- Pings (BOT-061, replies BOT-062): a bot that spots an enemy pings it (`kind: 'enemy'`, at most once per 4 s per bot); enemy pings from anyone share the position with the pinger's team bots; the team bot nearest a person's enemy ping replies 0.5..1.5 s later (`pingReply` bark). Bots also ping items: a team bot that picks an item goal pings `dibs` there (at most every `ITEM_PING_INTERVAL` 4 s per bot). On a mode call-out (capture, bomb, flag) the bots playing the objective bark it and, per team, the one nearest its objective pings it as `attack` or `defend` (`OBJECTIVE_PING_INTERVAL` 8 s per team). "Taking the point" is barked only by role bots that newly take a point or hill goal.
- Comm wheel (BOT-051, INP-003; `CommWheel` in 17g, an `Input` source): hold Comm (T) or hold Ping for 0.3 s; look input picks one of the 12 `COMM_CALLOUTS` (Attack, Defend, Cover me, Follow me, Hold position, Regroup, On my way, Dibs, No, Danger, Enemy, Go here) instead of turning the view (the look comes with each device's invert-Y applied, so the pick moves the way the view would); letting go sends `BotTeam.callout(player, index, to)`: a ping of that kind at the aimed spot (`at: 'aim'`; Enemy still tags an aimed enemy) or at the caller's feet (`at: 'self'`), to the team bot aimed at when the wheel opened (`to`, shown on the wheel) or the squad (`to: -1`). A reply (`at: 'reply'`) answers the newest teammate ping on the HUD (`HudPings.newestToAnswer`, 17d) with `BotTeam.reply(player, index, to, x, y, z)`: a ping at that spot, `to` its sender; with nothing to answer it is said at the caller's feet. Each row's `seconds` is its HUD marker life.
- The player's PING button (a tap shorter than 0.3 s, `BotTeam.playerPing`): an enemy within 4 deg of the aim line becomes an enemy ping; else the aimed spot (raycast, 120 m) becomes `defend` (own objective), `attack` (other objective) or `go`.
- `BotTeam.bark(id, category, now)`: at most one bark per bot per 5 s and per team per 1.5 s, spoken by the profile's chat chance; `reply` categories skip the chance and both limits; `chatter` off / minimal (only `minimal` categories) / normal; taunts off with `gameplay.taunts`. Returns true when spoken.
- Shopping (`BotEconomy`, `BOT_BUY`, bought through `Economy.buy`, so every mode rule on what a side may buy holds): once per buy time (round buy) or open wave shop. Round buy: a bot with less than `ecoMoney` (2000, x 0.75..1.25 by self-preservation) saves; else it buys a gun worth `upgrade` (0.1) more than its best, then armour, then a grenade (tiers with gadgets). Wave shop: refills low guns and, keeping `shopKeep` (300) credits, buys placed gadgets.
- Squads (BOT-052, `BOT_SQUAD`, versus modes, not `family: 'pve'` and not M20, which has its own squads in 13g/14e): at each commander round the bots of one role form squads of up to 3 in teamwork order, the first leads (`BotTeam.squadLeader[id]`: the leader, itself for a leader, -1 = none). Goal SQUAD (`BotTeam.squadPlan`, none while the bot has a target: fights come first): a follower walks to its leader's live position (radius `keep` 6 m), scoring 0.2 + 0.2 x teamwork (above roaming, below most items and fights); farther than `regroup` (15 m, its detour limit) it regroups at +0.25. A follower's detours (items, sounds) stay within `regroup` of the leader, and it values the zone its leader plays `SQUAD_ZONE_PULL` (1.5) times more. No squad goal while the leader is dead or downed; a killed leader hands the squad to its next follower (`passLead`) and rejoins it.
- Tokens (BOT-053, `BOT_TOKENS`): `BotTeam.takeGrenadeToken(id, now)` (one grenade per team every 3 s); `takeAttackToken(id, target, now)` (2 slots per target); `takeFlankToken(id, now)` (one per team, free-for-all bots one each). A holder renews its token at each decision; it frees when not renewed for `hold` (1.2 s), when the holder dies, or when the holder no longer plays ATTACK (CHASE for a flank).

### Debug view (14g)

- F9 (S18 toggles `developer.botDebug`): path points, destination and target markers (`Render.addSource('botDebug')`). On the HUD (`HudBatch.addLayer('botDebug', 90)`): the nav graph within 35 m of the camera (`Nav.debugLines` read once per map into a reused buffer, coloured by link type), each living bot's vision cone (the tier's field of view out to `viewRange`, orange once alerted), its alert meter and its awareness of the player as bars, and a label: name, tier, state / goal, role, the two best goal scores (text rebuilt 4 times a second).
- No allocation per frame; lines behind the camera are cut at the near limit. `BotDebug.stats` (`lines bars labels`) tells what the last pass drew.

## Debug commands (`__CTK`)

- `__CTK.bots`: `{ bots, sense, move, combat, decide, team, debug, difficulty }` plus `lastSoak` / `lastPerf` after the self-test.
- `__CTK.spawnBot(profile, team)`: adds and spawns a bot in the running match; returns its id or -1.
- `__CTK.setTier(id, tier)`: a test command (`DebugApi` in `20b-test-bots.js`); `Bots` has no `setTier`.
- `__CTK.soak(mode, map, minutes, botCount = 16, seed = 1, modeOptions = { timeLimit: 0, scoreLimit: 0 })`: plays a headless match (Ultra quality for 16 bots) for `minutes` or until it ends, and returns `{ mode, map, bots, seconds, botSeconds, stuckFraction, maxStuck, enemyStuck, enemyMaxStuck, nan, maxNoGoal, aiP95, aiMax, kills, shots, unknownShots, unperceivedShots, objectivesTouched, raysMax, ended, winner, reason, teamCaptures, actorCaptures, holdPoints }` (times in s, AI in ms per tick; `enemyStuck` and `enemyMaxStuck` = BotMove stuck seconds of `ACTOR_KIND.ENEMY` actors such as PvE enemies, while `stuckFraction` and `maxStuck` count every other actor, so a bot removed mid-match stays in the bots share; `teamCaptures` = team `capture` points, `actorCaptures` = per-actor capture credits, `holdPoints` = team `hold` points).
- `__CTK.bots.lastAccuracy` holds the per-tier hit rates of the self-test.
- `__CTK.bots.kits`: `{ play(batch, seconds, round), abilityBatches(rows), gadgetBatches(rows) }`, the kit matches of the tests (each row given to bots, meters kept full; returns the uses per row). `__CTK.bots.flagPush` is `BOT_FLAG_PUSH` (tune it live).

## Tests

- `bots` (default suite): data cross-checks, perception (smoke, cloak, a crouch-safe mine passed crouched, a prox mine walked around, a real frag run from and a smoke canister not, a real decoy), a frag landing within 4 m at two enemies and none at a lone one, flash from tier 3, smoke at a choke on an attacker's way (cycling from a frag to it), M20 squads following their leader (a fighting one only from 24 m) and respawning a squadmate at their beacon, the BOT-035 ultimate rule, the toggle off retry, flag rally rules, mutator and difficulty hooks, a 60 s match with 8 abilities (60% cast) and one with gadgets, short objective matches, a 1-minute soak on two maps, the easy-bot fight mix (three seeds pooled, because one 90 s match swings the shares by several points), the AI time budget.
- `botsoak` (slow: `--slow`, or `--group botsoak`): every ability and every gadget with a bot use is used at least once (sweeps over dom, the open test arena and a small room), all 15 modes for 120 s, CTF over 5 seeds (captures in 3+, both sides capture, neither side wins every match), and a 3-minute soak on every map, and the Warden check (the Warden spawn pistol scores at most 40% of Arena Classic kills on Foundry Spire and Harbor Nine).
