### 12.12 Acceptance tests for base specification requirements (AT, B)

One acceptance test per P0 and P1 requirement of spec part B, in matrix order (Part 15.1). Method is `auto: <UT/IT/FT/TT/PT/FI IDs>`, `manual: <XT or LT IDs>` or `review` (documentation and process obligations). A Method cell may combine forms, main form first, separated by `; `; every form in the cell is part of the acceptance test.
