## 11. Implementation roadmap: end-to-end table of contents

This part is the end-to-end table of contents required by B 1.1 and B 1.2 item 1, organized
as the seventeen phases of B 27. It is the executable roadmap of the implementing session:
Part 0.4 step 4 walks these entries in order, one T section at a time. Part 11 is split over
three files while the plan is drafted (11a phases 0-5, 11b phases 6-10, 11c phases 11-16); the
assembled document keeps one numbering.

Conventions for every entry:

1. Identifier. `T<pp>.<nn>`: `pp` is the two-digit phase (00-16), `nn` the step, contiguous
   from 01 in each phase. IDs are stable; an inserted step takes the next free number and the
   reordering is a LEDGER "Decisions" line (B 27 last rule, R-B27-20).
2. Labels. Every entry has the seven labels Position, Purpose, Covers, Depends on,
   Deliverables, Verification and Open items; with the heading they give the eight mandatory
   fields of B 1.1.
3. Covers. The R-IDs of Part 15 that the entry delivers, then the modules (M-IDs) it touches.
   An R-ID may appear in several entries; the entry that closes it last carries it in the
   Part 15 Phase column after the mechanical rewrite of 15.0.1.
4. Depends on. T-IDs that must be closed first. A dependency on a phase drafted in another
   file is written `Phase NN exit` (all entries of phase NN closed and its gate commit made).
5. Verification. The named tests must exist under their exact names and pass in a headless
   run after the entry's last change (Part 12.1.4). Every Verification line implicitly also
   includes `AT-<R-ID>` for each P0 and P1 R-ID on its Covers line; the AT IDs written out
   are the ones the entry's exit criterion depends on most directly. A test that a later
   entry completes is marked `(partial until Tpp.nn)` or `(partial until Phase NN exit)`
   and must pass in the form the current code supports. XT and LT tests are owner runs on
   physical devices (Part 12b); they become binding at the Phase 15 gate and are named here
   so that the owner knows which entry they validate.
6. Module presence. A source file is created by the entry that first lists its module.
   `FLM.Boot.EXPECTED_MODULES` (T01.10) lists the namespaces whose files exist; every entry
   that creates a module file appends its namespace in the same change. Boot steps and
   `FLM.Modes` enter or leave steps whose module is not yet expected are skipped and logged
   once (`Log.info('boot', 'skip <step>: <Namespace> not built')`); from Phase 16 nothing is
   skipped and a missing module is fatal (UT-29-04).
7. Names. Part 4 and Part 5 names are used. Where a Part 6 section names a member
   differently, the module exports the Part 4.1 name as an alias of the same function (rule
   of Part 6.28.9); entries name the Part 4.1 form.
8. Nothing in this part is implemented. Phase 0 entries marked "complete-by-this-document"
   refer only to parts of this plan; their re-validation happens at Phase 16.
