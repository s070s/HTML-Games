# Mizamon

An original, satirical monster-collecting role-playing game: Greece, 3000 AD. The creatures (Mizamon) are fictional corrupt-politician archetypes, plus two clearly marked parody characters. The satire targets behaviours and institutions: bribery, nepotism, red tape, media, police overreach, church wealth, influencer culture.

This is the MVP plus all six districts and the Βουλή (the finale): Πειραιάς with the town Νέα Πειραιάς, route 1, the rival, three route trainers, the port boss, then (after the Παράσημο Λιμανιού) route 2 (the quay road) with two more trainers and the town Καστέλλα (hospital, kiosk, theatre); and Νότια, the south coast: route 3 (Λεωφόρος Ποσειδώνος), the town Παλαιό Φάληρο (hospital, kiosk, café, ΚΤΕΛ stop), route 4 (the old airfield at Ελληνικό), the town Γλυφάδα (hospital, kiosk, ΚΤΕΛ stop, the café of the boss Ο Κύριος Μπουγάτσας), seven trainers and the Παράσημο Παραλιακής; and Ανατολικά, the east: route 5 (the Hymettus pass), the town Κορωπί (hospital, kiosk, café, ΚΤΕΛ stop), route 6 (the plain), the town Σπάτα (hospital, kiosk, ΚΤΕΛ stop and the airport terminal «Διαστημολιμένας» of the boss Η @influencεr_Φαίη), seven trainers and the Παράσημο Επιβίβασης; and Βόρεια, the leafy north: route 7 (the marble mountain Πεντέλη), the town Κηφισιά (hospital, kiosk, café, ΚΤΕΛ stop), route 8 (Λεωφόρος Κηφισίας, a boulevard with a roundabout), the town Μαρούσι (hospital, kiosk, ΚΤΕΛ stop and the concert stadium «Στάδιο Συναυλιών 3000» of the boss DJ Αμαρτία), seven trainers and the Παράσημο Πρώτης Σειράς; and Δυτικά, the dense west: route 9 (Ο Αυτοκινητόδρομος, a highway with toll booths), the town Περιστέρι (hospital, kiosk, café, ΚΤΕΛ stop, a street market and a football ground), route 10 (Λεωφόρος Θηβών, an avenue beside the river channel), the town Χαϊδάρι (hospital, kiosk, ΚΤΕΛ stop and the office of the «Μονή Αφορολογήτου» of the boss Ο Ηγούμενος Πλουσιάκης), seven trainers and the Παράσημο Αφορολογήτου; and Αθήνα, the centre: route 11 (Ιερά Οδός, the old paved road with a closed dig), the town Μοναστηράκι (hospital, kiosk, café, ΚΤΕΛ stop, the flea market, and the Rock above it where the legend Ο Τελευταίος Φορολογούμενος waits), route 12 (Λεωφόρος Πανεπιστημίου, an avenue with a round plaza), the town Σύνταγμα (hospital, kiosk, ΚΤΕΛ stop, the hill, the door of the Βουλή, and the boss Ο Ταξίαρχος Μπαμπούλας in the open square), eight trainers and the Παράσημο Συγκέντρωσης; and the Βουλή itself, behind the door of Σύνταγμα: a lobby with a doctor who heals between the fights, three chambers (Η Προϊσταμένη Ευτέρπη, Ο Υπουργός Σπυράδωνις, the rival Ο Μπέμπης), the plenary hall of Ο Υφυπουργός Παντός with Μιζοτάκης as his ace, the final badge Διορισμός Εφ’ Όρου Ζωής, the credits and a roof that saves the ending, and afterwards the legend Ηλιθιοκρατία in the emptied plenary hall. 38 Mizamon (the starters evolve at level 16 and again at 32, nine more creatures evolve at 20, 22, 26, 27, 30, 36 and 37), the Dossier (a fake police file), a shop, a hospital (heals the team, the return point after a black-out, and the Αρχείο Ασθενών that stores up to 60 extra Mizamon), a save point, a short "Chapter 1 complete" card and a bonus television battle.

The game is built from `src/` (the code) and `data/` (the content: creatures, moves, items, trainers, maps, texts, sounds, music) into one self-contained page, `Mizamon/build/index.html` (an assembly step with Node built-ins only and no install; the page needs no network). Requirements and decisions: the handoffs repo (`C:\Users\GS\Desktop\handoffs\Mizamon`: SPEC.md, TASKS.md, HANDOFF.md) and `REFERENCE-mizamon.md` here (every design decision with its reason, the formulas and the balance table).

`revengin/` holds the Owner's study ROM. It is gitignored and is studied for mechanics only; nothing from it goes into the game.

## Play
Open `Mizamon/build/index.html` in a browser (desktop or phone); make it first with `node Mizamon/tools/build.mjs`, or write a quick copy with `node Mizamon/tools/assemble.mjs --out <temp>/mizamon.html` (see Build). Greek is the default; Options switches to English. The game starts with the satire notice.

| | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | arrows / WASD | d-pad or left stick | on-screen d-pad |
| Confirm / talk | Z, Enter, Space | A | A button |
| Back | X, Escape, Backspace | B | B button |
| Menu | M, Tab | Start | MENU button |
| Map | Q | Select / Back | ΧΑΡΤΗΣ button |
| Mute | N | | Options |

- Walk in tall grass to meet independent (wild) Mizamon. A battle is a television debate: the two fighters stand on podiums, a poll bar (the HP) hangs over each, the commands are four tabs (Ομιλία = moves, Τσάντα, Κόμμα = switch, Διαφυγή) and the messages run in a news ticker at the bottom. In a battle, offer a Φακελάκι or Φάκελος from the Bag to recruit an independent one (odds rise when it is hurt, Υπό Έρευνα or Λαδωμένος).
- The Νοσοκομείο 3000 heals your team: talk to the receptionist (free "in 3 to 5 years", then at once; with a Φακελάκι you can pay for the VIP service). If your whole team loses you wake up there. The terminal on the left (Αρχείο Ασθενών) deposits and withdraws Mizamon; a recruit that does not fit in a full team of 6 goes to it by itself. The ΚΕΠ 3000 only has a queue. The Περίπτερο sells items. Saving happens at the Καφενείο.
- Beat Ο Λιμενάρχης Κώτσος at the port to get the Παράσημο Λιμανιού and see the "Chapter 1 complete" card. Afterwards the gate at the east end of route 1 opens (route 2 and Καστέλλα) and a Τηλεοπτικό Στούντιο opens in the square. Above level 13 a defeated creature gives more experience (x level / 13); the full credits come at the end of the game, after the Βουλή.
- Νότια: the promenade at the east end of Καστέλλα leads on (route 3, Παλαιό Φάληρο, route 4, Γλυφάδα). The starters evolve at level 16. The kiosks of Νότια also sell the Δικηγόρος (150: heals 80 HP and clears a status) and the Γεμιστά της Μάνας (300: full heal and clears a status). Beat Ο Κύριος Μπουγάτσας in the café «Η Μεγάλη Προκυμαία» for the Παράσημο Παραλιακής; with it the street at the east end of Γλυφάδα opens.
- Ανατολικά: route 5 (three switchbacks up the Hymettus ridge), Κορωπί, route 6 (a plain under a highway bridge) and Σπάτα, the airport town (its kiosks sell the same eight items as Νότια). Δημοσιοϋπαλληλάκι evolves at level 20, Story-ούλα at 22 and Κρυπτο-Μάκης at 26. Beat Η @influencεr_Φαίη in the terminal «Διαστημολιμένας» for the Παράσημο Επιβίβασης; with it the street at the east end of Σπάτα opens.
- Βόρεια: route 7 (white marble steps and cranes), Κηφισιά (hedged villas, a plane-tree square), route 8 (a boulevard of shop windows) and Μαρούσι (the arena complex). Λαϊκατζής evolves at level 27, Εφοπλιστάκος at 30, and the three starters get their last form at 32. The kiosks of Βόρεια also sell the Χοντρός Φάκελος (600: a recruit envelope stronger than the Φάκελος). Beat DJ Αμαρτία in the «Στάδιο Συναυλιών 3000» for the Παράσημο Πρώτης Σειράς; with it the street at the west end of Μαρούσι opens.
- Δυτικά: route 9 (four highway lanes, a toll plaza and crash barriers), Περιστέρι (apartment blocks with laundry, a market, a football ground), route 10 (a straight avenue with market stalls and a river channel) and Χαϊδάρι (the pass town). Καφενόβιος, Εφοράκι and Μοναστηριάκι evolve at level 36 and Γιαγιάκα at 37. The kiosks of Δυτικά also sell the Συλλογική Σύμβαση (150: heals 120 HP). Beat Ο Ηγούμενος Πλουσιάκης in the «Μονή Αφορολογήτου» for the Παράσημο Αφορολογήτου; with it the street at the west end of Χαϊδάρι opens.
- Αθήνα: route 11 (an ancient paved road between column stumps and closed digs), Μοναστηράκι (flea-market stalls, a clock tower, marble steps up to the Rock), route 12 (neoclassical fronts, a plaza round a fountain) and Σύνταγμα (police vans in rows, the closed metro entrance, the fountain, the shut door of the Βουλή). The kiosks sell the same ten items as Δυτικά. On the Rock the legend Ο Τελευταίος Φορολογούμενος (a level 40 creature, once only: beat it or recruit it, and it is gone) stands in front of the monument; the hill east of Σύνταγμα has wild creatures and a trainer. Beat Ο Ταξίαρχος Μπαμπούλας in the open square for the Παράσημο Συγκέντρωσης; with it the door of the Βουλή opens.
- Βουλή (levels 44-50, no wild creatures): four fights in a row, each chamber opens when the one before it is won. Only the doctor of the lobby heals, so go back out to him between the fights. The last fight (Ο Υφυπουργός Παντός, level 47-50, Μιζοτάκης at level 50) gives the Διορισμός Εφ’ Όρου Ζωής, rolls the credits and leaves you on the roof, where the café counter saves the game with its ending. In the emptied plenary hall the legend Ηλιθιοκρατία (level 50, once only) waits.
- The Χάρτης (Map) shows the six districts of Attica as a schematic, not to scale: open it from the menu or with Q, Select or the ΧΑΡΤΗΣ button; the red pin is where you are, the arrows move between the places you know, A shows the district sheet.
- Options: language, sound, text speed, Reduced motion (no shaking or flashing; the default follows the operating system setting) and an Accessibility screen (Options, row 5):
  - Large text (Off / Large / Largest): the text being read (the dialog page, the chosen menu row, the battle ticker) shows again in big letters in a bar under the picture; it is also a screen-reader live region.
  - Type marks (default on): a small shape beside every type colour (diamond, hourglass, ring, triangle, disc, three bars, triangle down), so the seven types can be told apart without colour.
  - Flashes off: a creature that is hit stays still and dim instead of shaking and blinking, the evolution glows steadily, and the cursors and the heart line stop blinking or changing colour.
  - Hold to repeat (Off / Slow / Normal / Fast): how a held arrow moves a menu cursor; Normal is the old timing, Off is one step per press.
  - Touch controls (Two hands / One hand: right / One hand: left): the one-hand layouts put all eight buttons on one side, within one thumb.
- The save is in the browser (`localStorage` key `mizamon.v1.save`, save format version 3; older saves are migrated). Without storage the game still plays; the save lasts only while the page is open.

## Test
- Headless rules tests (Node 24+, no installs): `node Mizamon/tools/run-tests.mjs` prints `PASS n / m` and exits 1 on a failure. It assembles the page from `src/` and `data/` in memory (or reads the file named by `MZ_HTML`) and loads the rules between the `// RULES-BEGIN` and `// RULES-END` lines (the data is inside that block, on the lines between `/*DATA-BEGIN*/` and `/*DATA-END*/`).
- The move of the content into `data/` is proved against a baseline in `tests/baseline/` (main at 1cbc9d6, see `BASELINE.txt`): the rules tables, the replay checksums, the `balance.mjs` output, the migrated saves and the sound call lists must stay equal (the tests named "DEV-02b parity"; `node Mizamon/tools/parity.mjs --write <folder> --root <old checkout>/Mizamon` made the files). `source-scans.json` holds how much every search of the page text found on the baseline: a test that searches the page must find at least that much, so a search that stops matching fails (`MZ_RECORD_SCANS=<file>` records a new baseline when run on a checkout).
- Balance tables (the first boss fight, the Πειραιάς XP band, the route 2 trainers, then Νότια, Ανατολικά, Βόρεια, Δυτικά and Αθήνα: the XP band, the boss, the seven trainers of each, eight in Αθήνα, then the Βουλή: its XP band, the four fights by seed and per starter, the legend Ηλιθιοκρατία): `node Mizamon/tools/balance.mjs [runs]`. While working, `MZ_TEST_FILTER=<regex>` runs only the rule tests whose name matches.
- World map picture (the six-district schematic, `Mizamon/docs/world-map.png`): `node Mizamon/tools/world-map.mjs` (`--ascii` prints the grid). The world design is section 11 of `REFERENCE-mizamon.md`.
- In the browser (UI smoke tests and the same rules checks): open `Mizamon/build/index.html`, then in the console `await MIZAMON.selfTest()` returns a list of `{ name, pass, detail }`. The accessibility checks in it work at any window size; the review runs them at 390x844, 844x390 and 1280x720.

## Build
- Sources (`Mizamon/src/`): `shell.html` (the page, the CSS and the script frame, with the lines `// RULES-BEGIN`, `/*@@DATA@@*/`, `/*@@RULES@@*/`, `// RULES-END` and `/*@@ENGINE@@*/`), `rules/NN-name.js` (the pure rules block; `02-data.js` builds today's tables from the data) and `engine/NN-name.js` (the browser code), and `engine-pinned.json` (the content ids the code still names on purpose; a test keeps it true). The content is in `Mizamon/data/` (section Data below). The fragments are plain script text in one shared scope (no `import`), joined in name order (`^\d\d[a-z]?-[a-z0-9-]+\.js$`) and cut at the section header comments of the old single file. A new fragment takes a number that puts it where its code belongs. `shared/assemble-core.mjs` does the joining, `tools/assemble.mjs` reads `src/`.
- `node Mizamon/tools/build.mjs [--check] [--no-tests] [--out <folder>] [--json]`: takes the build lock (`Mizamon/build/.lock`, one build at a time), assembles the page, runs the rule tests on it, then writes `Mizamon/build/index.html` and `Mizamon/build/build-report.json` (the source hash and the SHA-256 of `index.html`). `--check` writes nothing; `--no-tests` is a quick build whose report says "untested" and which the itch push refuses.
- `node Mizamon/tools/assemble.mjs --out <temp>/mizamon.html`: the quick look; no tests, nothing written in the repo.
- `Mizamon/build/` is gitignored except `build-report.json`, which is committed on main only. After a merge into main (or after the Owner's saved edits are committed), run `node Mizamon/tools/build.mjs` there and commit its report alone, with the message "Mizamon: build report". A task branch never commits anything under `Mizamon/build/`: the merge gate is that `git diff --name-only main...HEAD -- Mizamon/build` prints nothing. So parallel merges never conflict on the report, and the attributes `merge=binary` turn any slip into a conflict that is solved by building again.
- **Release after a merge into main: `node tools/release-mizamon.mjs`**, the one command (from the repo root, on main). It runs the steps below in order, stops at the first failure and says which one failed; the pushed commit is what players get, so nothing is uploaded from a working copy. The same steps as one line:
  ```
  test "$(git symbolic-ref --short HEAD)" = main && node ~/.claude/scripts/heavy.mjs -- node Mizamon/tools/build.mjs && test -z "$(git status --porcelain -- Mizamon/src Mizamon/data Mizamon/shared Mizamon/tools)" && git add -- Mizamon/build/build-report.json && git commit -q -m "Mizamon: build report" -- Mizamon/build/build-report.json && git push origin main && node tools/itch-push.mjs mizamon
  ```
  - `test "$(git symbolic-ref --short HEAD)" = main`: stops unless the current branch is main, so a release never pushes or uploads another branch's history.
  - `node ~/.claude/scripts/heavy.mjs -- node Mizamon/tools/build.mjs`: builds, runs the tests and writes the report; `heavy.mjs` waits for a free heavy-job slot first.
  - `test -z "$(git status --porcelain -- Mizamon/src Mizamon/data Mizamon/shared Mizamon/tools)"`: stops when any source is uncommitted, so the report describes the commit it is committed beside.
  - `git add -- Mizamon/build/build-report.json && git commit -q -m "Mizamon: build report" -- Mizamon/build/build-report.json`: commits the report alone.
  - `git push origin main`: the itch push reads `origin/main`, so the report commit must be pushed before the upload.
  - `node tools/itch-push.mjs mizamon`: checks both hashes against the report and uploads.

## Data
- `Mizamon/data/` is JSON, UTF-8 without BOM, LF, 2-space indent, keys in schema order, a final newline, written by one writer (`shared/json-format.mjs`; the schemas are in `shared/schemas.mjs`). A kind is a folder with `index.json` (the ordered list of ids) and one file per entry: `types/`, `statuses/`, `abilities/`, `moves/`, `species/`, `items/`, `shops/`, `trainers/`, `tiles/` (the id is `u` and the four hex digits of the tile character), `maps/`, `districts/` (with their places and routes), `events/`, `sounds/`, `music/`, and `strings/` (one file per group, the first part of the key: `key: { el, en }`). `game.json` holds the numbers of the rules, the ids the engine names (start spot, starters, the rival's answers, the finale spot, the music and sound roles) and `dataVersion`; `world.json` is the grid of the map screen. The field names are the ones the rules always had. What an ability or a status does is a kind of the rules code with its numbers in the data (`abilities/<id>.json` is `{ kind, params }`; the catalogue of kinds is `ABILITY_KINDS` and `STATUS_KINDS` in `shared/schemas.mjs`); the rival who leaves the map has a `hideFlag` on his NPC.
- The assembler (`tools/assemble.mjs`) reads `data/` in index order and writes one line `const DATA = {...};` between the lines `/*DATA-BEGIN*/` and `/*DATA-END*/`, with every `<` written as `<`; `src/rules/02-data.js` builds the tables the rules use from it. A data string that contains a marker text of the page (`// RULES-BEGIN`, `// RULES-END`, `/*DATA-BEGIN*/`, `/*DATA-END*/`, `/*@@`) is refused (`shared/validate.mjs`).
- To change content by hand: edit the entry's file (a new entry also needs its line in the kind's `index.json`), then `node Mizamon/tools/migrate-data.mjs --check` says whether every file is exactly what the writer writes (without `--check` it rewrites the files that are not). `dataVersion` in `game.json` is the shape of the files: data with a newer version than the code knows is refused, and a change to the shape raises it with a step in `tools/migrate-data.mjs`.

## Publish
`node tools/itch-push.mjs mizamon [--dry-run]` from the repo root (the last step of the release above; run it alone only when the report is already pushed; uploads the last pushed commit to `s070s/mizamon`). It never uploads the working copy: it reads the build report at `origin/main` (status must be "ok"), assembles that commit's own `src/` in a temporary git worktree under the OS temp folder (removed again at the end), and refuses unless the source hash and the SHA-256 of the assembled page equal the report's, so a merge without a new build is refused until the report is committed. `--ref <commit>` checks another commit (a local one, for a dry run), `--verify-only` stops before butler.
